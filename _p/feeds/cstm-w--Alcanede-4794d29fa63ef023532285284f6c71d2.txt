href=\"/wiki/Santar%C3%A9m_Municipality\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Santar\u00e9m Municipality\">Santar\u00e9m</a>, Portugal, in the district of the same name, of the <a href=\"/wiki/Ribatejo_Province\" title=\"Ribatejo Province\">Ribatejo</a>. The population in 2011 was 4,547,<sup id=\"cite_ref-ine_1-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-ine-1\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>1<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> in an area of 105.84&#160;km<sup>2</sup>.<sup id=\"cite_ref-dgt_2-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-dgt-2\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>2<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> Between 1163 and 1855 it was a municipal seat (with a population of 6408 inhabitants in 1801).\n</p>\n<meta property=\"mw:PageProp/toc\" />\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"History\">History</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Alcanede&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1\" title=\"Edit section: History\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<figure class=\"mw-halign-left\" typeof=\"mw:File/Thumb\"><a href=\"/wiki/File:Entrada_do_Castelo_de_Alcanede.JPG\" class=\"mw-file-description\"><img src=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ee/Entrada_do_Castelo_de_Alcanede.JPG/250px-Entrada_do_Castelo_de_Alcanede.JPG\" decoding=\"async\" width=\"170\" height=\"227\" class=\"mw-file-element\" srcset=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ee/Entrada_do_Castelo_de_Alcanede.JPG/330px-Entrada_do_Castelo_de_Alcanede.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ee/Entrada_do_Castelo_de_Alcanede.JPG/500px-Entrada_do_Castelo_de_Alcanede.JPG 2x\" data-file-width=\"2748\" data-file-height=\"3664\" /></a><figcaption>Entrance to the Castle of Alcanede</figcaption></figure>\n<p>Between 1163 and 1855, with a population around 6408 (1801), it was the municipal seat of a municipality consisting of Louriceira, Malhou, Alcobertas, Fr\u00e1guas, Abr\u00e3, Alcanede and Arneiro das Milhari\u00e7as.\n</p><p>During the Roman occupation, the region of Alcanede constituted the western limit of the Santar\u00e9m <i>colonia</i>; it was a secondary Roman transit roadway.\n</p><p><a href=\"/wiki/Afonso_Henriques\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Afonso Henriques\">Afonso Henriques</a> donated the village of Alcanede to Gon\u00e7alo Mendes de Sousa, while the ecclesiastical rights were transferred to the Monastery of Coimbra,<sup id=\"cite_ref-Pitoresco_3-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Pitoresco-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> in 1163, resulting in the construction of a religious temple that records referred to as <i>Santa Maria de Alcanede</i>.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Igreja_4-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Igreja-4\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>4<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> The first <a href=\"/wiki/Prior_(ecclesiastical)\" title=\"Prior (ecclesiastical)\">prior</a> was Father Fern\u00e3o Anes (1299).<sup id=\"cite_ref-Igreja_4-1\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Igreja-4\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>4<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>In 1179, the castle's garrison assisted Fuas Roupinho to defeat Moorish incursions and attacks on the Castle of Porto de M\u00f3s, and in 1187 King <a href=\"/wiki/Sancho_I_of_Portugal\" title=\"Sancho I of Portugal\">Sancho I</a> donated the castle in Alcanede to the militia in \u00c9vora (the future Order of Aviz).<sup id=\"cite_ref-Pitoresco_3-1\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Pitoresco-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>King <a href=\"/wiki/Ferdinand_I_of_Portugal\" title=\"Ferdinand I of Portugal\">Ferdinand</a> had the castle remodelled in 1370, at the same time while the men of Alcanede participated in the public works in the Castle of Santar\u00e9m.<sup id=\"cite_ref-5\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-5\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>5<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> In 1372, Vasco Fernandes de Cam\u00f5es (descendant of <a href=\"/wiki/Lu%C3%ADs_de_Cam%C3%B5es\" title=\"Lu\u00eds de Cam\u00f5es\">Lu\u00eds de Cam\u00f5es</a>) was appointed the <i>Alcaide-mor</i> of the Castle of Alcanede, just as the garrison allied itself with the Master of Aviz (per the chronicler Fern\u00e3o Lopes).\n</p><p>In 1514, King <a href=\"/wiki/Manuel_I_of_Portugal\" title=\"Manuel I of Portugal\">Manuel</a> provided a regal charter (<a href=\"/wiki/Portuguese_language\" title=\"Portuguese language\">Portuguese</a>: <i lang=\"pt\"><a href=\"/wiki/Foral\" title=\"Foral\">foral</a></i>). During the accession crisis, the municipality supported Philip II of Spain, yet for a while, <a href=\"/wiki/Ant%C3%B3nio,_Prior_of_Crato\" title=\"Ant\u00f3nio, Prior of Crato\">Ant\u00f3nio, Prior of Crato</a>, sought refuge in Alcanede, on his escape from the country. In July 1679, Father Ant\u00f3nio Vieira stayed in Alcanede, as revealed from his correspondence with diplomat Duarte Ribeiro de Macedo.\n</p><p>On 16 July 1719, the local population revolted against taxes imposed by the <a href=\"/wiki/Order_of_Aviz\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Order of Aviz\">Order of Aviz</a>, going as far as marching to Lisbon and receiving a favourable hearing from King John V.\n</p><p>During the third French invasion, between November 1810 and March 1811, the main village is occupied and pillaged by French troops from Massena's 8th Corp.\n</p><p>After the 1820 Liberal revolution, the Municipal Chamber of Alcanede aligned itself with the new regime. During the 1846 <a href=\"/wiki/Patuleia\" title=\"Patuleia\">Patuleia</a>, Alcanede and its region were sites of many confrontations between <i>Cartistas</i> and <i>Septembrist</i> forces.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Geography\">Geography</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Alcanede&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2\" title=\"Edit section: Geography\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Alcanede is a parish of the district and municipality of <a href=\"/wiki/Santar%C3%A9m_Municipality\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Santar\u00e9m Municipality\">Santar\u00e9m</a>, situated 26 kilometres from the regional capital. It is bordered by its former parishes: <a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Gan%C3%A7aria&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1\" class=\"new\" title=\"Gan\u00e7aria (page does not exist)\">Gan\u00e7aria</a>, <a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Abr%C3%A3&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1\" class=\"new\" title=\"Abr\u00e3 (page does not exist)\">Abr\u00e3</a>, <a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Abitureiras&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1\" class=\"new\" title=\"Abitureiras (page does not exist)\">Abitureiras</a> and <a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Trem%C3%AAs&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1\" class=\"new\" title=\"Trem\u00eas (page does not exist)\">Trem\u00eas</a>. The parish contains the localities Aldeia de Al\u00e9m, Aldeia da Ribeira, Alqueid\u00e3o do Mato, Alqueid\u00e3o do Rei, Bairro dos Murtais, Barreirinhas, Casais da Charneca, Casal de Al\u00e9m, Cov\u00e3o dos Porcos, Espinheira, Mata do Rei, Mosteiros, Murteira, Prado, P\u00e9 da Pedreira, <a href=\"/wiki/Vale_da_Trave\" title=\"Vale da Trave\">Vale da Trave</a>, Vale do Carro, Vale do href=\"/w/index.php?title=Alcanede&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3\" title=\"Edit section: Economy\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>The agricultural community, in addition to being supported by domestication of herd species, is involved in meat processing, along with the extraction of <a href=\"/wiki/Limestone\" title=\"Limestone\">limestone</a>, forestry and saw-milling, construction, bread-making and small commercial businesses.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Alcanede&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5\" title=\"Edit section: Civic\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<ul><li>Roman Bridge/Medieval</li>\n<li>Dinosaur footprints</li></ul>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading3\"><h3 id=\"Military\">Military</h3><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Alcanede&amp;action=edit&amp;section=6\" title=\"Edit section: Military\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<ul><li><b>Castle of Alcanede</b> - dating to the <a href=\"/wiki/Roman_Empire\" title=\"Roman Empire\">Roman</a> epoch, it was situated on an ancient <a href=\"/wiki/Castro_culture\" title=\"Castro culture\">castro</a>, which was expanded and renovated during the high <a href=\"/wiki/Middle_Ages\" title=\"Middle Ages\">Middle Ages</a>.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Castelo_6-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Castelo-6\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>6<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> Conquered from the Moors by Afonso Henriques in 1091 during his campaigns in Santar\u00e9m and Lisbon, the castle was commanded by Gon\u00e7alo Mendes de Sousa, who renovated and reconstructed the walled citadel, in addition to settling and organizing the village.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Castelo_6-1\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Castelo-6\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>6<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> During the reign of <a href=\"/wiki/Dinis_of_Portugal\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Dinis of Portugal\">Dinis</a> the village and castle passed into the hands of the <a href=\"/wiki/Order_of_Aviz\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Order of Aviz\">Order of Aviz</a>, resulting in new remodelling, with the construction of the <a href=\"/wiki/Keep\" title=\"Keep\">keep</a>.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Castelo_6-2\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Castelo-6\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>6<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> The loss of its military importance, directly affected the village's importance; the monarchy considered the reconstruction of ruined castle (affected by the 1755 earthquake) as a possible solution to the region's problems (which the Order of Aviz supported). By 1926, the fort was in an advance stage of ruin; in 1941, the <i>DGEMN - Direc\u00e7\u00e3o Geral dos Edif\u00edcios e Monumentos Nacionais</i> (<a href=\"/wiki/Portuguese_language\" title=\"Portuguese language\">Portuguese</a>: <i lang=\"pt\">General Directorate of Buildings and National Monuments</i>) initiated public works to reconstruct the aged structure, which lasted nine years, and involved reconstruction of many of the walls, towers and interior spaces.</li></ul>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading3\"><h3 id=\"Religious\">Religious</h3><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Alcanede&amp;action=edit&amp;section=7\" title=\"Edit section: Religious\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<ul><li><b>Church of Senhora da Concei\u00e7\u00e3o</b> (<a href=\"/wiki/Portuguese_language\" title=\"Portuguese language\">Portuguese</a>: <i lang=\"pt\">Lady of Conception</i>) - the Matriz Church, originally the Church of <i>Santa Maria de Alcanede</i>, it was constructed in 1163 by Gon\u00e7alo de Sousa, and consists of a church with one nave, wooden roof, along with arched main and lateral chapels that were elaborated in the late part of the 17th century during extensive renovations that included <a href=\"/wiki/Azulejo\" title=\"Azulejo\">azulejo</a> tiling of the pulpit.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Igreja_4-2\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Igreja-4\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>4<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> Similar tiles were completed in the 18th century, followed by exterior walls around 1874, and extensive interior remodelling.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Igreja_4-3\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Igreja-4\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>4<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> A <a href=\"/wiki/Manualine\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Manualine\">Manualine</a>-style <a href=\"/wiki/Baptistery\" title=\"Baptistery\">baptistery</a> located alongside the church was completed during the first half of the 16th century, and includes a simple baptismal fountain accessed through a Manueline archway.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Igreja_4-4\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Igreja-4\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>4<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>\n<li>Chapel of Miseric\u00f3rdia</li></ul>\n<div class=\"mw-heading
