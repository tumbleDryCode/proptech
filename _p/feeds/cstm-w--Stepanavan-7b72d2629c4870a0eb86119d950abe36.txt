href=\"http://stepanavan.am\">stepanavan.am</a></td></tr></tbody></table>\n<p><b>Stepanavan</b> (<a href=\"/wiki/Armenian_language\" title=\"Armenian language\">Armenian</a>: <span lang=\"hy\">\u054d\u057f\u0565\u0583\u0561\u0576\u0561\u057e\u0561\u0576</span>), is a town and municipal community in the <a href=\"/wiki/Lori_Province\" title=\"Lori Province\">Lori Province</a> of <a href=\"/wiki/Armenia\" title=\"Armenia\">Armenia</a>. It is located 139&#160;km north of the capital <a href=\"/wiki/Yerevan\" title=\"Yerevan\">Yerevan</a> and 24&#160;km north of the provincial centre <a href=\"/wiki/Vanadzor\" title=\"Vanadzor\">Vanadzor</a>, halfway between Yerevan and Tbilisi. As of the 2011 census, the population of the town is 13,086. Currently, the town has an approximate population of 10,800 as per the 2016 official estimate.\n</p><p>Due to its location on the shores of <a href=\"/wiki/Dzoraget_River\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Dzoraget River\">Dzoraget River</a> among the forest of Lori plateau and Bazum mountains, Stepanavan is considered a resort town in northern Armenia.\n</p>\n<meta property=\"mw:PageProp/toc\" />\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Etymology\">Etymology</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Stepanavan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1\" title=\"Edit section: Etymology\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Stepanavan was founded in 1810 as <i>Jalaloghli</i>, literally meaning <i>son of Jalal</i> in Turkic languages. The name is derived from the Armenian noble family of <a href=\"/wiki/House_of_Hasan-Jalalyan\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"House of Hasan-Jalalyan\">Hasan-Jalalyan</a>. A khachkar-memorial in the town testifies that the settlement was founded in 1810 by prince <i>Davit Hasan-Jalalyan</i>. In 1923, Jalaloghli was renamed <i>Stepanavan</i>, meaning <i>town of Stepan</i> in Armenian, in honour of the bolshevik leader <a href=\"/wiki/Stepan_Shaumian\" title=\"Stepan Shaumian\">Stepan Shaumian</a>.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"History\">History</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Stepanavan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2\" title=\"Edit section: History\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<figure class=\"mw-default-size mw-halign-left\" typeof=\"mw:File/Thumb\"><a href=\"/wiki/File:%D4%B2%D5%A5%D6%80%D5%A4%D5%AB_%D5%B0%D5%A1%D5%BF%D5%BE%D5%A1%D5%AE%D5%B6%D5%A5%D6%80.jpg\" class=\"mw-file-description\"><img src=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/de/%D4%B2%D5%A5%D6%80%D5%A4%D5%AB_%D5%B0%D5%A1%D5%BF%D5%BE%D5%A1%D5%AE%D5%B6%D5%A5%D6%80.jpg/220px-%D4%B2%D5%A5%D6%80%D5%A4%D5%AB_%D5%B0%D5%A1%D5%BF%D5%BE%D5%A1%D5%AE%D5%B6%D5%A5%D6%80.jpg\" decoding=\"async\" width=\"220\" height=\"293\" class=\"mw-file-element\" srcset=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/de/%D4%B2%D5%A5%D6%80%D5%A4%D5%AB_%D5%B0%D5%A1%D5%BF%D5%BE%D5%A1%D5%AE%D5%B6%D5%A5%D6%80.jpg/330px-%D4%B2%D5%A5%D6%80%D5%A4%D5%AB_%D5%B0%D5%A1%D5%BF%D5%BE%D5%A1%D5%AE%D5%B6%D5%A5%D6%80.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/de/%D4%B2%D5%A5%D6%80%D5%A4%D5%AB_%D5%B0%D5%A1%D5%BF%D5%BE%D5%A1%D5%AE%D5%B6%D5%A5%D6%80.jpg/440px-%D4%B2%D5%A5%D6%80%D5%A4%D5%AB_%D5%B0%D5%A1%D5%BF%D5%BE%D5%A1%D5%AE%D5%B6%D5%A5%D6%80.jpg 2x\" data-file-width=\"1536\" data-file-height=\"2048\" /></a><figcaption>The remains of the fortress dating back to the 3rd millennium BC</figcaption></figure>\n<p>The area of modern-day Stepanavan was first settled during the 4th millennium BC. The remains of an ancient fortress is found in the western edge of the town dating back to the 3rd millennium BC. Later, it became part of the <a href=\"/wiki/Urartu\" title=\"Urartu\">Urartu</a> Kingdom between the 8th and 6th centuries BC. After the <a href=\"/wiki/Achaemenid_Empire\" title=\"Achaemenid Empire\">Achaemenid</a> invasion, the region became part of the <a href=\"/wiki/Satrapy_of_Armenia\" title=\"Satrapy of Armenia\">Satrapy of Armenia</a> between the 6th and 4th centuries BC. With the establishment of the <a href=\"/wiki/Kingdom_of_Armenia_(antiquity)\" title=\"Kingdom of Armenia (antiquity)\">Kingdom of Armenia</a> in 331 BC, the region became part of the <i><a href=\"/wiki/Tashir_(historical_region)\" title=\"Tashir (historical region)\">Tashir</a></i> canton of <a href=\"/wiki/Gugark\" title=\"Gugark\">Gugark</a>; the 13th province of Greater Armenia.\n</p><p>Following the partition of Armenia in 387 between the <a href=\"/wiki/Byzantine_Empire\" title=\"Byzantine Empire\">Byzantine Empire</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Sassanid_Persia\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Sassanid Persia\">Sassanid Persia</a>, and the subsequent collapse of <a href=\"/wiki/Arsacid_dynasty_of_Armenia\" title=\"Arsacid dynasty of Armenia\">Arsacid Armenia</a> in 428, <a href=\"/wiki/Eastern_Armenia\" title=\"Eastern Armenia\">Eastern Armenia</a> including Tashir region became under the rule of Sassanid Persia. However, the earliest records about human settlement in modern-day Stepanavan dates back to the 5th century, when the basilica of the Holy Mother of God was built on the shores of <a href=\"/wiki/Dzoraget_River\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Dzoraget River\">Dzoraget River</a> (destroyed by the Soviets during the 1930s).\n</p><p>During the 2nd half of the 7th century, Armenia was conquered by the <a href=\"/wiki/Umayyad_Caliphate\" title=\"Umayyad Caliphate\">Arab</a> invaders. At the end of the 9th century, Tashir became part of the newly established <a href=\"/wiki/Bagratid_Armenia\" title=\"Bagratid Armenia\">Bagratid Kingdom of Armenia</a>. In 979, King <a href=\"/wiki/Kiurike_I\" title=\"Kiurike I\">Kiurike I</a> founded the <a href=\"/wiki/Kingdom_of_Tashir-Dzoraget\" title=\"Kingdom of Tashir-Dzoraget\">Kingdom of Tashir-Dzoraget</a> (alternatively known as the Kingdom of Lori) under the rule of the Kiurikian dynasty and the protectorate of the <a href=\"/wiki/Bagratuni_dynasty\" title=\"Bagratuni dynasty\">Bagratid kings of Armenia</a>. The Kiurikians ruled the kingdom until 1118 when Tashir-Dzoraget became part of the <a href=\"/wiki/Kingdom_of_Georgia\" title=\"Kingdom of Georgia\">Kingdom of Georgia</a>.\n</p>\n<figure 2x\" data-file-width=\"960\" data-file-height=\"720\" /></a><figcaption>The 13th century chapel of the Holy Saviour</figcaption></figure>\n<p>The <a href=\"/wiki/Seljuk_Empire\" title=\"Seljuk Empire\">Seljuks</a> invaded the region in the early 12th century, but their rule did not last long and in 1118-1122 the Georgian king <a href=\"/wiki/David_the_Builder\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"David the Builder\">David the Builder</a> conquered Lori and granted the rule to the Georgian-Armenian <a href=\"/wiki/Orbelian_Dynasty\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Orbelian Dynasty\">Orbelian Dynasty</a>. The Orbelians revolted unsuccessfully in 1177, after which a <a href=\"/wiki/Kipchaks\" title=\"Kipchaks\">Kipchak</a> named Khubasari was appointed <i><a href=\"/wiki/Amirspasalari\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Amirspasalari\">spasalari</a></i> of Lori. Later in 1185, the province became ruled by the <a href=\"/wiki/Zakarid-Mkhargrzeli\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Zakarid-Mkhargrzeli\">Zakarian dynasty</a> after Queen <a href=\"/wiki/Tamar_of_Georgia\" title=\"Tamar of Georgia\">Tamar of Georgia</a> appointed the Zakarid prince Sarkis as its governor.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Matevossian_2-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Matevossian-2\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>2<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Holy Saviour chapel dating back to the 13th century is still found at the centre of modern-day Stepanavan.<sup id=\"cite_ref-3\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> Located on the northern trade route, Tashir region turned into a significant commercial centre in medieval Armenia. However, the region was devastated by the <a href=\"/wiki/Mongol_invasions_of_Georgia\" title=\"Mongol invasions of Georgia\">Mongol invasion of 1236</a> led by <a href=\"/wiki/Chaghatai_Khan\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Chaghatai Khan\">Chaghatai Khan</a>, and the Zakarian dynasty declined by the second half of the 14th century.\n</p><p>Tashir region was annexed by <a href=\"/wiki/Safavid_Persia\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Safavid Persia\">Safavid Persia</a> as a result of the 1555 <a href=\"/wiki/Peace_of_Amasya\" title=\"Peace of Amasya\">Peace of Amasya</a> and became part of Persia's Kartli-Kakheti province. After <a href=\"/wiki/Nader_Shah\" title=\"Nader Shah\">Nader Shah</a>'s murder in 1747, the Georgian kingdoms of <a href=\"/wiki/Kingdom_of_Kartli_(1484%E2%80%931762)\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Kingdom of Kartli (1484\u20131762)\">Kartli</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Kingdom_of_Kakheti\" title=\"Kingdom of Kakheti\">Kakheti</a> became independent and united into a <a href=\"/wiki/Kingdom_of_Kartli-Kakheti\" title=\"Kingdom of Kartli-Kakheti\">single kingdom</a> by 1762.<sup id=\"cite_ref-4\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-4\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>4<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> In 1801, together with the Georgian provinces of Kartli and Kakheti, Tashir was annexed by the <a href=\"/wiki/Russian_Empire\" title=\"Russian Empire\">Russian Empire</a>. During the reign of <a href=\"/wiki/Catherine_II_of_Russia\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Catherine II of Russia\">Catherine the Great</a>,<sup class=\"noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact\" style=\"white-space:nowrap;\">&#91;<i><a href=\"/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed\" title=\"Wikipedia:Citation needed\"><span title=\"This claim needs references to reliable sources. (December 2016)\">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> the town of Jalaloghly was founded in 1810, by the Armenian prince <a href=\"/wiki/House_of_Hasan-Jalalyan\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"House of Hasan-Jalalyan\">Davit Hasan-Jalalyan</a> from <a href=\"/wiki/Artsakh_(historic_province)\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Artsakh (historic province)\">Artsakh</a>. Tashir became officially part of the Russian Empire at the <a href=\"/wiki/Treaty_of_Gulistan\" title=\"Treaty of Gulistan\">Treaty of Gulistan</a> signed on 1 January 1813 between Imperial Russia and <a href=\"/wiki/Qajar_Persia\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Qajar Persia\">Qajar Persia</a>.<sup id=\"cite_ref-5\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-5\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>5<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> Administratively, Jalaloghli was part of the <a href=\"/wiki/Borchaly_uezd\" title=\"Borchaly uezd\">Borchaly uezd</a> within the <a href=\"/wiki/Tiflis_Governorate\" title=\"Tiflis Governorate\">Tiflis Governorate</a> of the Russian Empire.\n</p>\n<figure class=\"mw-default-size\" typeof=\"mw:File/Thumb\"><a 2x\" data-file-width=\"3194\" data-file-height=\"2173\" /></a><figcaption>The statue of Stepan Shaumian</figcaption></figure>\n<p>In May 1918, Lori became part of the newly formed <a href=\"/wiki/First_Republic_of_Armenia\" title=\"First Republic of Armenia\">Republic of Armenia</a>. In late 1918, <a href=\"/wiki/First_Republic_of_Armenia\" title=\"First Republic of Armenia\">Armenia</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_Georgia\" title=\"Democratic Republic of Georgia\">Georgia</a> fought a <a href=\"/wiki/Georgian%E2%80%93Armenian_War\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Georgian\u2013Armenian War\">border war</a> over Lori. In January 1919, the Lori neutral zone was established by the British forces. Following Armenia's sovietization in December 1920, Lori -including Jalaloghli- was finally incorporated into <a href=\"/wiki/Soviet_Armenia\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Soviet Armenia\">Soviet Armenia</a> on 11 February 1921.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Matevossian_2-1\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Matevossian-2\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>2<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> In 1923, Jalaloghli was renamed Stepanavan after the Armenian Bolshevik leader <a href=\"/wiki/Stepan_Shaumian\" title=\"Stepan Shaumian\">Stepan Shaumian</a>.\n</p><p>In 1930, Stepanavan became the centre of the newly formed <i>Stepanavan raion</i>. The first major plan of the town was introduces in 1957. It was revised in 1962 and 1971. Stepanavan was granted the status of a town of republican subordination in 1967. Originally built on the right bank of the <a href=\"/wiki/Dzoraget_River\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Dzoraget River\">Dzoraget River</a>, Stepanavan has been expanded towards the right bank of the river as well after the devastating <a href=\"/wiki/1988_Armenian_earthquake\" title=\"1988 Armenian earthquake\">1988 earthquake</a> that left around 6000 families homeless in the town.<sup id=\"cite_ref-6\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-6\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>6<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> The 2 parts of the town are connected with each other through the Stepanavan Bridge opened in 1989.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Geography\">Geography</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Stepanavan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3\" title=\"Edit section: Geography\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Stepanavan is situated on Lori plateau to the north of Bazum mountains, on the shores of river <a href=\"/wiki/Dzoraget_(river)\" title=\"Dzoraget (river)\">Dzoraget</a> at an average height of 1375 meters above sea level, covering an area of 17&#160;km<sup>2</sup>. The deep gorge of Dzoraget divides Stepanavan into northern and southern banks, the latter of which has deeper historical roots.\n</p><p>Surrounded with thick forests and alpine meadows, Stepanavan has a relatively humid climate with an average yearly temperature of 6.6 C. The average temperature of January is -4,2 C while in July it makes +16,7 C. The annual precipitation is 683&#160;mm with snowy winters.\n</p><p>Stepanavan is a health resort known for its <a href=\"/wiki/Pine\" title=\"Pine\">pine</a> forests and mountainous climate. The <a href=\"/wiki/Essential_oils\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Essential oils\">essential oils</a>, isolated by <a href=\"/wiki/Coniferous\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Coniferous\">coniferous</a> trees, possess strong <a href=\"/wiki/Bactericidal\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Bactericidal\">bactericidal</a> properties; therefore mountainous air of Stepanavan, filled with the aroma of pine tree, is useful for patients who suffer from <a href=\"/wiki/Respiratory\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Respiratory\">respiratory</a> problems.\n</p>\n<figure class=\"mw-halign-center\" typeof=\"mw:File/Thumb\"><a href=\"/wiki/File:Arjasar,_Stepanavan_05.jpg\" class=\"mw-file-description\"><img src=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/11/Arjasar%2C_Stepanavan_05.jpg/800px-Arjasar%2C_Stepanavan_05.jpg\" decoding=\"async\" width=\"800\" height=\"110\" class=\"mw-file-element\" srcset=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/11/Arjasar%2C_Stepanavan_05.jpg/1200px-Arjasar%2C_Stepanavan_05.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/11/Arjasar%2C_Stepanavan_05.jpg/1600px-Arjasar%2C_Stepanavan_05.jpg 2x\" data-file-width=\"12470\" data-file-height=\"1711\" /></a><figcaption><div class=\"center\" style=\"width:auto; margin-left:auto; margin-right:auto;\">Mount Arjasar (1819 meters) overlooking the town of Stepanavan from its south</div></figcaption></figure>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading3\"><h3 id=\"Climate\">Climate</h3><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Stepanavan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4\" title=\"Edit section: Climate\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Stepanavan has a <a href=\"/wiki/Humid_continental_climate\" title=\"Humid continental climate\">humid continental climate</a> with <i>Dfb</i> designation in <a href=\"/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification\" title=\"K\u00f6ppen climate classification\">K\u00f6ppen climate classification</a>. The town is connected with other municipalities in Lori vi\u0430 the H-34 Road from the north, H-33 Road from the east and H-23 Road from the west.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Economy_and_tourism\">Economy and tourism</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Stepanavan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=9\" title=\"Edit section: Economy and tourism\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<figure class=\"mw-default-size\" typeof=\"mw:File/Thumb\"><a href=\"/wiki/File:Stepanavan_Dendropark_1,_ArmAg_(2).jpg\" class=\"mw-file-description\"><img src=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4b/Stepanavan_Dendropark_1%2C_ArmAg_%282%29.jpg/220px-Stepanavan_Dendropark_1%2C_ArmAg_%282%29.jpg\" decoding=\"async\" width=\"220\" height=\"165\" class=\"mw-file-element\" srcset=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4b/Stepanavan_Dendropark_1%2C_ArmAg_%282%29.jpg/330px-Stepanavan_Dendropark_1%2C_ArmAg_%282%29.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4b/Stepanavan_Dendropark_1%2C_ArmAg_%282%29.jpg/440px-Stepanavan_Dendropark_1%2C_ArmAg_%282%29.jpg 2x\" data-file-width=\"4896\" data-file-height=\"3672\" /></a><figcaption><a href=\"/wiki/Stepanavan_Dendropark\" title=\"Stepanavan Dendropark\">Stepanavan Dendropark</a></figcaption></figure>\n<p>The light industry of the town is mainly based on food-processing and dairy products.\n</p><p>Stepanavan was one of Armenia's most famous tourist spots during the Soviet era, but it had suffered major destruction during the <a href=\"/wiki/1988_Armenian_earthquake\" title=\"1988 Armenian earthquake\">Spitak earthquake</a> of 1988. Nowadays, the city is reviving and tourists from all over the world visit Stepanavan to enjoy the beauty of one of Armenia's most picturesque towns. Nowadays, Stepanavan has many restaurants, hotels and sanatoriums that attract both local and foreign visitors.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading3\"><h3 id=\"Attractions\">Attractions</h3><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Stepanavan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=10\" title=\"Edit section: Attractions\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<ul><li><a href=\"/wiki/Dzoraget_River\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Dzoraget River\">Dzoraget Canyon</a>, a popular recreational destination.</li>\n<li>Lake Kyubishev near Stepanavan, hidden in the heart of Lori's alpine meadows. The lake is covered with lilies and bulrushes.</li>\n<li><a href=\"/wiki/Stepanavan_Dendropark\" title=\"Stepanavan Dendropark\">Stepanavan Dendropark</a>, founded in 1931 by \"Edmon Leonovich\", this 35-hectare (86-acre) botanical garden features a broad range of tree species from many regions. It is only 12&#160;km (7&#160;mi) west of Stepanavan, just outside the village of <a href=\"/wiki/Gyulagarak\" title=\"Gyulagarak\">Gyulagarak</a>. The park is known for its pine trees in \"Sochut\" area.</li></ul>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Education\">Education</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Stepanavan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=11\" title=\"Edit section: Education\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>The first school in Stepanavan was opened in 1869. Currently, there are 6 public education schools and 4 kindergartens in Stepanavan.\n</p><p>The Stepanavan branch of the <a href=\"/wiki/Crisis_Management_State_Academy\" title=\"Crisis Management State Academy\">Crisis Management State Academy</a> is operating since 2006, under the supervision of the Ministry of Emergency Situations of Armenia.<sup id=\"cite_ref-9\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-9\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>9<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Sport\">Sport</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Stepanavan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=12\" title=\"Edit section: Sport\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Football (soccer) is the most popular sport in Stepanavan. However, other types of sports including martial arts, winter sports and handball are also popular in the town.\n</p><p>Stepanavan was chosen as the youth and sport capital of 2015 in Armenia.<sup id=\"cite_ref-10\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-10\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>10<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Notable_people\">Notable people</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Stepanavan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=13\" title=\"Edit section: Notable people\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<ul><li><a href=\"/wiki/Avetik_Sahakyan\" title=\"Avetik Sahakyan\">Avetik Sahakyan</a> (1863\u20131933), politician, the first Parliament chairman of the <a href=\"/wiki/First_Republic_of_Armenia\" title=\"First Republic of Armenia\">First Republic of Armenia</a><sup class=\"noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact\" style=\"white-space:nowrap;\">&#91;<i><a href=\"/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed\" title=\"Wikipedia:Citation needed\"><span title=\"This claim needs references to reliable sources.
