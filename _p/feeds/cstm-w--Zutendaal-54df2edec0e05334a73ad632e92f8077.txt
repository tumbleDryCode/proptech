.IPA-label-small,.mw-parser-output .navbox .IPA-label-small{font-size:100%}</style><span class=\"IPA-label IPA-label-small\">Dutch pronunciation:</span> <span class=\"IPA nowrap\" lang=\"nl-Latn-fonipa\"><a href=\"/wiki/Help:IPA/Dutch\" title=\"Help:IPA/Dutch\">&#91;\u02c8zyt\u0259(n)\u02ccda\u02d0l&#93;</a></span>; <a href=\"/wiki/Limburgish_language\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Limburgish language\">Limburgish</a>: <i lang=\"li\">Zietendaol</i>; in English sometimes also referred to as \"Sweet Valley\") is a <a href=\"/wiki/Municipalities_of_Belgium\" title=\"Municipalities of Belgium\">municipality</a> located in the <a href=\"/wiki/Belgium\" title=\"Belgium\">Belgian</a> province of <a href=\"/wiki/Limburg_(Belgium)\" title=\"Limburg (Belgium)\">Limburg</a>. On 1 January 2017 Zutendaal had a total population of 7,269. The total area is 32.07&#160;km<sup>2</sup>, giving a <a href=\"/wiki/Population_density\" title=\"Population density\">population density</a> of 227 inhabitants per km<sup>2</sup>.<sup id=\"cite_ref-2\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-2\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>2<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>In addition to Zutendaal itself, the municipality includes the following population centres: Gewaai, Papendaal, Besmer, Broek, Stalken, Roelen, Daal, and Wiemesmeer.\n</p>\n<meta property=\"mw:PageProp/toc\" />\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Etymology\">Etymology</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Zutendaal&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1\" title=\"Edit section: Etymology\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>The first written mention of Zutendaal was in 1292 under the name of <i>Suerbroeck</i>, indicating that the land around the village was acidic and swampy. From 1345 the name changed to <i>Zuetendael</i> which literally translates to \"Sweet Valley\". Indicating there were a lot of freshwater sources around the village.<sup id=\"cite_ref-3\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"History\">History</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Zutendaal&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2\" title=\"Edit section: History\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>While the oldest settlements such as Papendaal, Zutendaal, Broek, Stalken and Roelen arose at the respective creek springs, Wiemesmeer, Gewaai and Besmer were founded much later, on the <a href=\"/wiki/Campine\" title=\"Campine\">Kempen plateau</a>. The Borough of Besmer was transferred from Lanaken to Zutendaal in the 19th century.\n</p><p>The first church is said to have been founded as early as the 7th century from the Abbey of Munsterbilzen. In 1304, the patronage right was transferred by Count Arnold V of Loon to the <a href=\"/wiki/Averbode_Abbey\" title=\"Averbode Abbey\">Abbey of Averbode</a>. South of the church, around the town square the <i>Vijverplein</i>, some remnants of the former village center remain.\n</p><p>During the 16th and 17th centuries, there were many problems with itinerant army bands. In 1598, the church of Zutendaal was set on fire and also later the population had to resort to entrenchments to get to safety from these army bands. In 1790, a battle took place on the hill the Hesselsberg between the Li\u00e8ge revolutionaries and the troops of the <a href=\"/wiki/Electoral_Palatinate\" title=\"Electoral Palatinate\">Elector of Pfaltz</a>, who supported the <a href=\"/wiki/Prince-Bishopric_of_Li%C3%A8ge\" title=\"Prince-Bishopric of Li\u00e8ge\">Prince-Bishop of Li\u00e8ge</a>.\n</p><p>At the end of the 19th and beginning of the 20th century, the heath was afforested for the production of mining wood. In 1938, on the local road Heiwijkerweg, the <a href=\"/wiki/FN_Herstal\" title=\"FN Herstal\">FN explosives factory</a> was established. In 1944 the Americans built the <a href=\"/wiki/Zutendaal_Air_Base\" title=\"Zutendaal Air Base\">Zutendaal Air Base</a>. On January 1, 1945 <a href=\"/wiki/Operation_Bodenplatte\" title=\"Operation Bodenplatte\">Operation Bodenplatte</a> raged here, which was lost by the Germans. From 1953 a NATO reserve base established itself at the air base.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Economy_and_tourism\">Economy and tourism</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Zutendaal&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3\" title=\"Edit section: Economy and tourism\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Today Zutendaal is one of the most densely forested municipalities of <a href=\"/wiki/Flanders\" title=\"Flanders\">Flanders</a>, with tourism being a source of income. The community is famous for its barefoot path at the Lieteberg, one of the gateways to the <a href=\"/wiki/Hoge_Kempen_National_Park\" title=\"Hoge Kempen National Park\">Hoge Kempen National Park</a> which nearly completely surrounds the municipality. Further economic activity can be found in some sand and gravel quarries.\n</p><p>Zutendaal is part of the <a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Albert_Canal_Economic_Network&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1\" class=\"new\" title=\"Albert Canal Economic Network (page does not exist)\">Albert Canal Economic Network</a> together with 24 other municipalities through which the canal runs.<sup id=\"cite_ref-4\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-4\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>4<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2
