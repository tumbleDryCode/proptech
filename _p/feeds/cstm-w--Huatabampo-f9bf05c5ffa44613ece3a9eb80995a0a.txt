.references .IPA-label-small,.mw-parser-output .infobox .IPA-label-small,.mw-parser-output .navbox .IPA-label-small{font-size:100%}</style><span class=\"IPA-label IPA-label-small\">Spanish pronunciation:</span> <span class=\"IPA nowrap\" lang=\"es-Latn-fonipa\"><a href=\"/wiki/Help:IPA/Spanish\" title=\"Help:IPA/Spanish\">&#91;wata\u02c8\u03b2ampo&#93;</a></span>) is a <a href=\"/wiki/City\" title=\"City\">city</a> in <a href=\"/wiki/Huatabampo_Municipality\" title=\"Huatabampo Municipality\">Huatabampo Municipality</a>  in the <a href=\"/wiki/List_of_states_of_Mexico\" title=\"List of states of Mexico\">state</a> of <a href=\"/wiki/Sonora\" title=\"Sonora\">Sonora</a>, in northwestern <a href=\"/wiki/Mexico\" title=\"Mexico\">Mexico</a>. It is situated on the <a href=\"/wiki/Gulf_of_California\" title=\"Gulf of California\">Gulf of California</a>, near the mouth of the <a href=\"/wiki/Mayo_River,_Mexico\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Mayo River, Mexico\">Mayo River</a>. It is located at latitude <span class=\"geo-inline\"><link rel=\"mw-deduplicated-inline-style\" href=\"mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1156832818\" /><span class=\"plainlinks nourlexpansion\"><a class=\"external text\" href=\"https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Huatabampo&amp;params=26_49_N_109_40_W_\"><span class=\"geo-default\"><span class=\"geo-dms\" title=\"Maps, aerial photos, and other data for this location\"><span class=\"latitude\">26\u00b049\u2032N</span> <span class=\"longitude\">109\u00b040\u2032W</span></span></span><span class=\"geo-multi-punct\">&#xfeff; / &#xfeff;</span><span class=\"geo-nondefault\"><span class=\"geo-dec\" title=\"Maps, aerial photos, and other data for this location\">26.817\u00b0N 109.667\u00b0W</span><span style=\"display:none\">&#xfeff; / <span class=\"geo\">26.817; -109.667</span></span></span></a></span></span>. Huatabampo is 34&#160;km (21&#160;mi) southwest of <a href=\"/wiki/Navojoa\" title=\"Navojoa\">Navojoa</a> via <a href=\"/wiki/Sonora_State_Highway_56\" title=\"Sonora State Highway 56\">Sonora State Highway 56</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Sonora_State_Highway_149\" title=\"Sonora State Highway 149\">Sonora State Highway 149</a>. <a href=\"/wiki/Mexican_Federal_Highway_15\" title=\"Mexican Federal Highway 15\">Mexican Federal Highway 15</a> can be accessed via <a href=\"/wiki/Sonora_State_Highway_176\" title=\"Sonora State Highway 176\">Sonora State Highway 176</a>. It is notable as the home of revolutionary general <a href=\"/wiki/%C3%81lvaro_Obreg%C3%B3n\" title=\"\u00c1lvaro Obreg\u00f3n\">\u00c1lvaro Obreg\u00f3n</a>, a successful chickpea farmer before the <a href=\"/wiki/Mexican_Revolution\" title=\"Mexican Revolution\">Mexican Revolution</a>, and now his burial site.\n</p>\n<meta property=\"mw:PageProp/toc\" />\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Etymology\">Etymology</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Huatabampo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1\" title=\"Edit section: Etymology\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>The name Huatabampo is from the local <a href=\"/wiki/Mayo_language\" title=\"Mayo language\">Mayo language</a>: \"Huata\" (Willow) + \"Bampo\" (Water), or \"Willow in/near the Water\".<sup id=\"cite_ref-1\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-1\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>1<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>Huatabampo is also known as \"Tierra de Generales\" (Land of Generals) since during the <a href=\"/wiki/Mexican_Revolution\" title=\"Mexican Revolution\">Mexican Revolution</a> (1910\u20131917) several high-ranking revolutionary generals emerged from the town.<sup id=\"cite_ref-2\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-2\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>2<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> Among them is <a href=\"/wiki/%C3%81lvaro_Obreg%C3%B3n\" title=\"\u00c1lvaro Obreg\u00f3n\">\u00c1lvaro Obreg\u00f3n</a> (1880\u20131928), who was born in <a href=\"/wiki/Navojoa\" title=\"Navojoa\">Navojoa</a>, but lived for many years in Huatabampo, the only undefeated general in the war. He was elected <a href=\"/wiki/President_of_the_Mexican_Republic\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"President of the Mexican Republic\">President of the Mexican Republic</a> (1920\u20131924) after the war, being an important link between the war-devastated country and the first stages of political, economical and social development. He was assassinated in 1928 after his election to a second term, before he could take office. He is buried in the old local cemetery. \n</p><p>Other generals from the area were General Jos\u00e9 Tiburcio Otero Toledo (1834\u20131900), a famous military and a governor of Sonora, and General\nIgnacio Otero Pablos (1896\u20131970), who was Ambassador to the Dominican Republic and Venezuela and also a candidate for <a href=\"/wiki/Governor_of_Sonora\" title=\"Governor of Sonora\">governor of Sonora</a>.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Demographics\">Demographics</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Huatabampo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2\" title=\"Edit section: Demographics\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>The population was 74,533 in 2005, with 29,789 inhabitants living in the municipal seat.  Other towns are Ejido la Uni\u00f3n, <a href=\"/wiki/Yavaros\" title=\"Yavaros\">Yavaros</a>, Sahuaral de Otero, Etchoropo, Huatabampito, Moroncarit, Agiabampo, Estaci\u00f3n Luis, <a href=\"/wiki/Las_Bocas,_Sonora\" title=\"Las Bocas, Sonora\">Las Bocas</a>, El Caro, Citavaro, Pozo Dulce, and El J\u00fapare. \n</p><p>As of 2005 the <a href=\"/wiki/Per_capita_income\" title=\"Per capita income\">per capita income</a> for the municipality of Huatabampo was $5,984 and the <a href=\"/wiki/Human_Development_Index\" title=\"Human Development Index\">Human Development Index</a> was 0.8002. <sup id=\"cite_ref-3\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Economy\">Economy</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Huatabampo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3\" title=\"Edit section: Economy\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Huatabampo is a major <a href=\"/wiki/Agriculture\" title=\"Agriculture\">agricultural</a> producer for the area, its produce includes <a href=\"/wiki/Chickpea\" title=\"Chickpea\">chickpeas</a> as well as assorted <a href=\"/wiki/Fruit\" title=\"Fruit\">fruit</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Vegetable\" title=\"Vegetable\">vegetable</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Cereal\" title=\"Cereal\">cereal</a> crops.  Cattle and swine raising is also very important.  \n</p><p>Since Huatabampo has 120&#160;km (75&#160;mi) of coastline, fishing plays a major role in the economy.  There are over 3,000 registered fishermen and around 20 open sea trawlers, in addition to almost 1,000 small boats.  Shrimp raising has also become a major industry in recent years. \n</p><p>In 2000 there were 8 industries for industrialization of sardines, crab, and shrimp.  The production of fish oil and fish flour is also important. \n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Tourism\">Tourism</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Huatabampo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4\" title=\"Edit section: Tourism\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>The city attracts a considerable number of <a href=\"/wiki/Tourism\" title=\"Tourism\">tourists</a>, primarily from the <a href=\"/wiki/United_States\" title=\"United States\">United States</a>, due to its <a href=\"/wiki/Beach\" title=\"Beach\">beaches</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Destination_spa\" title=\"Destination spa\">spas</a>.<sup class=\"noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact\" style=\"white-space:nowrap;\">&#91;<i><a href=\"/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed\" title=\"Wikipedia:Citation needed\"><span title=\"Which tourists? (October 2011)\">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>\n</p><p>Huatabampo is home to the Museum of <a href=\"/wiki/%C3%81lvaro_Obreg%C3%B3n\" title=\"\u00c1lvaro Obreg\u00f3n\">\u00c1lvaro Obreg\u00f3n</a> at the house of the revolutionary general and President of Mexico.
