class=\"external text\" href=\"http://www.patos.pb.gov.br/\">www.patos.pb.gov.br</a> <span class=\"languageicon\">(in Portuguese)</span></td></tr></tbody></table>\n<p><b>Patos</b> is a <a href=\"/wiki/Municipality_(Brazil)\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Municipality (Brazil)\">municipality</a> of the <a href=\"/wiki/States_of_Brazil\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"States of Brazil\">state</a> of <a href=\"/wiki/Para%C3%ADba\" title=\"Para\u00edba\">Para\u00edba</a> in the <a href=\"/wiki/Northeast_Region,_Brazil\" title=\"Northeast Region, Brazil\">Northeast Region</a> of <a href=\"/wiki/Brazil\" title=\"Brazil\">Brazil</a>. It is classified by the <a href=\"/wiki/Brazilian_Institute_of_Geography_and_Statistics\" title=\"Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics\">Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics</a> as a <a href=\"/wiki/Urban_hierarchy_in_Brazil#Sub-regional_centers\" title=\"Urban hierarchy in Brazil\">sub-regional center A</a>.<sup id=\"cite_ref-ibge_regic_1-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-ibge_regic-1\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>1<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id=\"cite_ref-ipardes_diana_2-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-ipardes_diana-2\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>2<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>It is located in the <a href=\"/wiki/Espinharas_River\" title=\"Espinharas River\">Espinharas River</a> valley, surrounded by the <a href=\"/wiki/Borborema_Plateau\" title=\"Borborema Plateau\">Borborema Plateau</a> to <a href=\"/wiki/East\" title=\"East\">east</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/South\" title=\"South\">south</a>, and by the <a href=\"/wiki/Pediplain\" title=\"Pediplain\">pediplain</a> <a href=\"/wiki/Sert%C3%A3o\" title=\"Sert\u00e3o\">Sertanejo</a> to the <a href=\"/wiki/West\" title=\"West\">west</a>. It originated from the village of Patos, spun off from the <a href=\"/wiki/Pombal,_Para%C3%ADba\" title=\"Pombal, Para\u00edba\">Parish of Nossa Senhora do Bom Sucesso de Pombal</a> on October 6, 1788.<sup id=\"cite_ref-3\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>The city is 306&#160;km from the city of <a href=\"/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_Pessoa,_Para%C3%ADba\" title=\"Jo\u00e3o Pessoa, Para\u00edba\">Jo\u00e3o Pessoa</a>, the center of its <a href=\"/wiki/List_of_Intermediate_and_Immediate_Geographic_Regions\" title=\"List of Intermediate and Immediate Geographic Regions\">immediate and intermediate geographic regions</a>. It stands out as an educational, commercial, banking, religious and health center, both in the <a href=\"/wiki/Sert%C3%A3o\" title=\"Sert\u00e3o\">back country of Para\u00edba</a>, and in areas of <a href=\"/wiki/Pernambuco\" title=\"Pernambuco\">Pernambuco</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Rio_Grande_do_Norte\" title=\"Rio Grande do Norte\">Rio Grande do Norte</a>.<sup id=\"cite_ref-4\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-4\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>4<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is the third most important municipality in the state considering the economic, political and social aspects (behind Jo\u00e3o Pessoa and <a href=\"/wiki/Campina_Grande\" title=\"Campina Grande\">Campina Grande</a>).<sup id=\"cite_ref-5\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-5\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>5<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> According to IBGE estimates for 2021, it is the fourth most populous municipality in the state with 108,766 inhabitants. <sup id=\"cite_ref-IBGE_Pop_2021_6-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-IBGE_Pop_2021-6\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>6<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p><a href=\"/wiki/Esporte_Clube_de_Patos\" title=\"Esporte Clube de Patos\">Esporte</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Nacional_Atl%C3%A9tico_Clube_(Patos)\" title=\"Nacional Atl\u00e9tico Clube (Patos)\">Nacional</a> are the city's two <a href=\"/wiki/Association_football\" title=\"Association football\">football</a> (soccer) clubs. They play at the Jos\u00e9 Cavalcanti Municipal Stadium. There are four multisport arenas: Rivald\u00e3o, AABB, SESC and SESI.\n</p><p>The city is served by <a href=\"/wiki/Patos_Airport\" title=\"Patos Airport\">Brig. Firmino Ayres Airport</a>.\n</p><p><br />\n</p>\n<figure class=\"mw-default-size mw-halign-none\" typeof=\"mw:File/Thumb\"><a href=\"/wiki/File:Patos12.jpg\" class=\"mw-file-description\"><img src=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8e/Patos12.jpg/220px-Patos12.jpg\" decoding=\"async\" width=\"220\" height=\"165\" class=\"mw-file-element\" srcset=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8e/Patos12.jpg/330px-Patos12.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8e/Patos12.jpg/440px-Patos12.jpg 2x\" data-file-width=\"2816\" data-file-height=\"2112\" /></a><figcaption>Patos, Brazil</figcaption></figure>\n<meta property=\"mw:PageProp/toc\" />\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Climate\">Climate</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Patos&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1\" title=\"Edit section: Climate\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>The city of Patos experiences a <a href=\"/wiki/Tropical\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Tropical\">tropical</a> <a href=\"/wiki/Semi-arid_climate#Hot_semi-arid_climates\" title=\"Semi-arid climate\">hot semi-arid climate</a> (<a href=\"/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification\" title=\"K\u00f6ppen climate classification\">K\u00f6ppen</a>: <i>BSh</i>), just short of a <a href=\"/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate\" title=\"Tropical savanna climate\">tropical savanna climate</a>, with a short rainy season from February to April. During this period, when the equatorial rainband associated with the highly seasonalized positioning of the <a href=\"/wiki/Intertropical_Convergence_Zone\" title=\"Intertropical Convergence Zone\">Intertropical Convergence Zone</a> is over the city, warm to hot temperatures and abundant equatorial rainfall prevail. Conversely, the dry season dominates the remaining majority of the year, with abundant sunshine prevalent from May to January. The wettest month is March, with an average monthly precipitation of 213&#160;mm (8.38&#160;in), while the driest month is September, with an average precipitation of only 1&#160;mm
