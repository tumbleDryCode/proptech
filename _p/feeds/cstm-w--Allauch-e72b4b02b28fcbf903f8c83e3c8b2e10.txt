It is a part of the <a href=\"/wiki/Aix-Marseille-Provence_Metropolis\" title=\"Aix-Marseille-Provence Metropolis\">Aix-Marseille-Provence Metropolis</a>.\nThe commune had 21,372 inhabitants as of 2019.\n</p><p>It is located 12&#160;km north-east of <a href=\"/wiki/Marseille\" title=\"Marseille\">Marseille</a>, 10&#160;km north-east of <a href=\"/wiki/Aubagne\" title=\"Aubagne\">Aubagne</a> and 35&#160;km south of <a href=\"/wiki/Aix-en-Provence\" title=\"Aix-en-Provence\">Aix-en-Provence</a>. Access to the commune is by the D908 road from Marseille in the south-west passing through the commune and continuing north to join the D96 north of <a href=\"/wiki/La_Bouilladisse\" title=\"La Bouilladisse\">La Bouilladisse</a>. Access to the village is by the D48 branching off the D908 and looping back as the D48A. There is also the D4A road from the 11th arrondissement in the south, and the D44G from the 12th arrondissement in the south-west.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Google_3-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Google-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>As Allauch is located on the edge of the Marseille conurbation there are numerous urban districts located within the commune. class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Allauch&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4\" title=\"Edit section: Urbanism\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Allauch Village is located on the north-eastern fringe of the <a href=\"/wiki/Marseille\" title=\"Marseille\">Marseille</a> conurbation with large parts of the west of the commune also forming part of this conurbation joining with <a href=\"/wiki/Plan-de-Cuques\" title=\"Plan-de-Cuques\">Plan-de-Cuques</a> to the west.\n</p><p>Allauch is separated from <a href=\"/wiki/Mimet\" title=\"Mimet\">Mimet</a> by the massif of l'\u00c9toile, resulting in it being necessary to detour to <a href=\"/wiki/Cadolive\" title=\"Cadolive\">Cadolive</a> in order to go from Allauch to <a href=\"/wiki/Mimet\" title=\"Mimet\">Mimet</a>. Similarly it is separated from the <a href=\"/wiki/Aubagne\" title=\"Aubagne\">Aubagne</a> urban area by the Garlaban hills to the south-east. Apart from the urban area in the west of the commune, the rest is heavily forested and mountainous.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading3\"><h3 id=\"Housing\">Housing</h3><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Allauch&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5\" title=\"Edit section: Housing\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Each year Allauch pays about \u20ac200,000 for missing social housing in order to reach the 20% required by the Urban Renewal Law (SRU).<sup id=\"cite_ref-SRU_4-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-SRU-4\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>4<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> According to the commune however, available land is limited: of the 5,032 hectares of the commune a total of 4,000 is conservation land, 500 are already urbanized and the balance is sometimes affected by risks (flooding, collapse, fire, etc.). Recent increases in the price of land suitable for building has resulted in increasing difficulties in growing the social housing stock.\n</p><p>The City Council has decided that each construction project should have between 20% and 30% social housing. The first obstacle is that the commune owns little suitable land in built-up areas to build social housing (near schools, shops, services, transport, etc. to avoid creating \"ghettos\"). The value of the land is very high and the commune must sell land to social housing operators at well below the actual value. Furthermore, the municipality must guarantee 50% of the borrowings and contribute financially up to \u20ac20,000 per unit. The procedures are slow and it can take 3 or 4 years from the sales agreement and delivery of the first housing.\n</p><p>Another obstacle is that, in 2006, 108 applications for social housing (Social Housing loans) were made by the commune but only 48 were granted by the urban community to which the state has delegated its powers, while the State requires the construction of 64 units per year.\n</p><p>According to the Abbe Pierre Foundation for Disadvantaged Housing, of 319 housing units the commune should have built between 2002 and 2006 it has neither built nor funded any,<sup id=\"cite_ref-abb\u00e9-pierre_5-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-abb\u00e9-pierre-5\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>5<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> something that Allaudiens were far from disapproving having re-elected the Povinelli list of candidates by 81.15% in the local elections in 2008.\n</p><p>The commune may have another opportunity to fulfil its obligation by purchasing existing housing through preemption to transform social housing.<sup id=\"cite_ref-appartements_6-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-appartements-6\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>6<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading3\"><h3 class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Allauch&amp;action=edit&amp;section=12\" title=\"Edit section: History\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>One of the objects found in the Baume Sourno Cave (dark cave) may have been used as a wedding gift for the young Gyptis.<sup id=\"cite_ref-10\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-10\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>10<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> The history of Marseille has a story that Marseille was born from the union of a handsome Greek sailor and the daughter of King Nann, the chief of a <a href=\"/wiki/Ligures\" title=\"Ligures\">Ligurian</a> tribe around the year 600 BC. However, historians believe that Allauch was the chief town of a Ligurian tribe called s\u00e9gobrige. The beautiful Gyptis could have been allaudienne and Marseille girl from Allauch. In <a href=\"/wiki/Provence\" title=\"Provence\">Provence</a> and in Allauch in particular people like legends.\n</p><p>A Coat of Arms was adopted by the town in 1727 and refers to the commune being the seat for the <a href=\"/wiki/Saracens\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Saracens\">Saracens</a>: in the 10th century the <a href=\"/wiki/Moors\" title=\"Moors\">Moors</a> besieged the village. At the brink of starvation the villagers, to deceive the enemy, sent twenty loaves and their remaining spear points. The Saracens were discouraged, imagining that abundance reigned behind the walls. They broke camp. It was night and the moon was in its last quarter with three stars shone around it. So the arms of Allauch have three stars, a crescent moon, and two silver wings (arrow quills).\n</p><p>Under the auspices of the canons of the <i>Cathedral de la Major Marseille</i>, the castle was built in the 12th century. There remains a postern, some ramparts, and Our Lady of the Castle which was built in 1148 . The pride of Allauch, it is still a place of worship and pilgrimage. The canons remained the Spiritual and Temporal Lords for nearly seven centuries until the <a href=\"/wiki/French_Revolution\" title=\"French Revolution\">French Revolution</a>. They attracted a new population seeking land to cultivate. Then began a period of clearing the land and covering it with vineyards, olive groves, and wheat.\n</p><p>On the eve of the Revolution more than three-quarters of Allauch soil belonged to the peasants who worked it, about 20% by the nobles and bourgeois, and only 3% by the clergy. Throughout the 19th century Allauch declined: Agriculture and livestock stagnated as did the production of plaster and chalk extracted using traditional methods.\n</p><p>In contrast to the early 19th century, during the suppression of the free port of Marseille, Allauch was the scene of contraband tobacco. Probably practiced initially by only a few it soon became generalized and became the main industry of Allauch during the century<sup id=\"cite_ref-contrebande_11-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-contrebande-11\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>11<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> ahead of the stone quarries, the plasterworks, and the bauxite deposits.\n</p><p>The construction of the <a href=\"/wiki/Canal_de_Marseille\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Canal de Marseille\">Canal de Marseille</a> started a recovery: running water arrived in Allauch in 1888. Then an electric tram line connecting Marseille to Plan-de-Cuques came in 1902 followed by a connection to Bourdonni\u00e8re and Allauch via Pounche in 1908: it was the end of isolation and confirmation of a new prosperity to come.\n</p><p>In the Decree of 25 March 1966 Allauch was classed as an urban commune and today Allauch has become increasingly a residential commune.<sup id=\"cite_ref-mus\u00e9e_d&#39;Allauch_12-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-mus\u00e9e_d&#39;Allauch-12\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>12<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup class=\"noprint Inline-Template\" style=\"white-space:nowrap;\">&#91;<i><a href=\"/wiki/Wikipedia:Citing_sources\" title=\"Wikipedia:Citing sources\"><span title=\"Statement needs to be more specific about the content to which it refers. (October 2020)\">specify</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading3\"><h3 id=\"Heraldry\">Heraldry</h3><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span id=\"Quality_of_life\">Quality of life</h3><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Allauch&amp;action=edit&amp;section=17\" title=\"Edit section: Quality of life\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>In the 1960s a study by <i>Agam</i> predicted population figures of 60 to 80,000 in Allauch in 2000. Today the commune has about 20,000 inhabitants. This is the result of political will of the various mayors who have never ceased to protect the hills, to restrict urbanisation, and contained demographic change.<sup id=\"cite_ref-plaquette_14-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-plaquette-14\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>14<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>According to the municipality, Allauch is an \"art town\" of greenery on the outskirts of Marseille with two-thirds of its territory consisting of hills while of the 119 communes in the department Allauch came 22nd highest for the housing tax (19.71% municipal rate in 2007) and 6th highest for the property tax (33.24% municipal rate in 2007).<sup id=\"cite_ref-minefi_15-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-minefi-15\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>15<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>The politics of the commune management of land use plan has both limited building areas in order not to distort the landscape and allowed only normal sized housing so that there would be sound economic development respecting the environment and refusing any infrastructure that denaturalises Allauch (highways, supermarkets, industries creating nuisances etc.)<sup id=\"cite_ref-macdo_16-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-macdo-16\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>16<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> and saving natural areas and green belts.\n</p><p>There is in place a protection service for the hills with an annual budget of \u20ac350,000 (15 water tanks, 2,000 hours annually to monitor the start of any fires), clearing obligations, and reforestation.<sup id=\"cite_ref-reboisement_17-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-reboisement-17\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>17<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>In the vote on joining the <a href=\"/wiki/Urban_Community_of_Marseille_Provence_M%C3%A9tropole\" title=\"Urban Community of Marseille Provence M\u00e9tropole\">Urban Community of Marseille Provence M\u00e9tropole</a>, Allauch commune voted against for fear of no longer having a voice regarding the land use plan.<sup id=\"cite_ref-vote_contre_18-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-vote_contre-18\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>18<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> The joining of the urban community in 2000 may have reduced the decision-making powers of the commune in urban planning matters,<sup id=\"cite_ref-logements_sociaux_19-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-logements_sociaux-19\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>19<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> highways, or street cleanliness by moving the decision-making centre but will perhaps achieve the construction of a school in Allauch.\n</p>\n<div The school bus is free. Nearly 1,500 meals are prepared daily in the Allauch central kitchen and transported in heated units to the schools. Each student entering Grade 2 undergoes dental screening and receives a dictionary provided by the municipality. Sports instructors and lifeguards are involved in each class so that children can engage in physical activity provided by a professional. Prevention sessions and training are provided by the Departmental Educational Committee for Health.\n</p><p>Since 1994 Allauch has had a college attended by 950 students. 2017 out of 9,136 Allaudiens who were employed, 21.5% worked in the commune and 78.5% elsewhere. 80.9% travelled to work by car.<sup id=\"cite_ref-complet_25-3\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-complet-25\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>25<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>Of the 3,661 jobs in the commune (2017), 47.1% were in the public sector (administration, education, health, social work) and 39.4% in trade and transport.<sup id=\"cite_ref-complet_25-4\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-complet-25\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>25<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>The Business Park of Fontvieille established in 1981 is the heart of the economic life of the commune. The criterion for acceptable settlement by the council is based on respect for the living environment by installing activities which do not create a nuisance or pollution.\n</p><p>The main activities fall into two divisions: food (nougat, caf\u00e9s) and small tech industries.\n</p><p>Near the Business Park the Laphal laboratory, a pharmaceutical company of international renown, provides more than 130 jobs in the commune. It has attracted many companies with complementary activities (e.g. IPP Pharma).\n</p><p>Allauch has over a hundred shops spread over three main urban centres: <i>le Village</i>, Logis Neuf, and Pounche: food, health and hygiene, home furnishings, bars, restaurants, and services. A shopping mall was recently opened in Fontvieille.\n</p><p>Arts and crafts also have a place especially during the Christmas market: ironwork, pottery, glassware, fabric, and figurines.\n</p><p>Allauch has two <i>G\u00eetes de France</i> (homestays), 9 tourist apartments, one 3 star hotel, one 2 star hotel, two 1 star hotels, a guesthouse, and a residential hotel complex. There is no municipal campsite.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading3\"><h3 id=\"Local_products\">Local products</h3><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Allauch&amp;action=edit&amp;section=30\" title=\"Edit section: Local products\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>The craftsmen from the commune make black and white nougat, tooth snacks (hard biscuits), the <i>suce-miel</i> (sucking honey) made as chewing gum has been made for a hundred years.\n</p><p>One family who converted to agriculture produce different <a href=\"/wiki/Goat_cheese\" title=\"Goat cheese\">goat cheeses</a> and fresh or refined <a href=\"/wiki/Raw_milk\" title=\"Raw milk\">raw milk</a>.\n</p><p>In January during the festival of gastronomy some fifty craftsmen and farmers hold a market: there are <a href=\"/wiki/Honey\" title=\"Honey\">honey</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Olive_oil\" title=\"Olive oil\">olive oil</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Appellation_d%27origine_contr%C3%B4l%C3%A9e\" title=\"Appellation d&#39;origine contr\u00f4l\u00e9e\">AOC</a> C\u00f4tes de Provence wine, Navettes de Marseille (Proven\u00e7al pastries), <a href=\"/wiki/Tapenade\" title=\"Tapenade\">Tapenade</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Gibassier\" title=\"Gibassier\">Gibassier</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Calisson\" title=\"Calisson\">Calisson</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Eggplant_salads_and_appetizers\" title=\"Eggplant salads and appetizers\">Eggplant salads and appetizers</a> on sale.\n</p><p>Allauch is also known for its <a href=\"/wiki/Santon_(figurine)\" title=\"Santon (figurine)\">Santons</a> (figurines).\n</p>\n<div
