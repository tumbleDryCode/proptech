language\">Arabic</a>: <span lang=\"ar\" dir=\"rtl\">\u0623\u0648\u0644\u0641</span>, <small><a href=\"/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic\" title=\"Romanization of Arabic\">romanized</a>:&#160;</small><span title=\"Arabic-language romanization\"><i lang=\"ar-Latn\">Awlaf</i></span>) is a town and <a href=\"/wiki/Communes_of_Algeria\" title=\"Communes of Algeria\">commune</a> and capital of <a href=\"/wiki/Aoulef_District\" title=\"Aoulef District\">Aoulef District</a>, in <a href=\"/wiki/Adrar_Province\" title=\"Adrar Province\">Adrar Province</a>, south-central <a href=\"/wiki/Algeria\" title=\"Algeria\">Algeria</a>. According to the 2008 census it has a population of 21,723,<sup id=\"cite_ref-census2008_1-1\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-census2008-1\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>1<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> up from 15,229 in 1998,<sup id=\"cite_ref-2\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-2\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>2<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> with an annual growth rate of 3.7%.<sup id=\"cite_ref-census2008_1-2\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-census2008-1\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>1<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p>\n<meta property=\"mw:PageProp/toc\" />\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Geography\">Geography</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Aoulef&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1\" title=\"Edit section: Geography\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>The town of Aoulef lies at an elevation of 288 metres (945&#160;ft) on the eastern side of an <a href=\"/wiki/Oasis\" title=\"Oasis\">oasis</a>. The oasis is a part of the <a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Tidikelt&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1\" class=\"new\" title=\"Tidikelt (page does not exist)\">Tidikelt</a> region of the central Sahara that extends from central eastern Adrar Province (where Aoulef is located) to northeast <a href=\"/wiki/Tamanrasset_Province\" title=\"Tamanrasset Province\">Tamanrasset Province</a> around <a href=\"/wiki/In_Salah\" title=\"In Salah\">In Salah</a>. The oases in this region are sparser than in the <a href=\"/wiki/Tuat\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Tuat\">Tuat</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Gourara\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Gourara\">Gourara</a> regions in which most of the population of Adrar Province live, but there are still a number of towns such as <a href=\"/wiki/Timokten\" title=\"Timokten\">Timokten</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Akabli\" title=\"Akabli\">Akabli</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Tit,_Adrar\" title=\"Tit, Adrar\">Tit</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/In_Ghar\" title=\"In Ghar\">In Ghar</a>. The nearby land is mostly flat aside from some small hills to the east; however further north the land rises to the <a href=\"/wiki/Tadema%C3%AFt\" title=\"Tadema\u00eft\">Tadema\u00eft</a> plateau.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Climate\">Climate</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Aoulef&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2\" title=\"Edit section: Climate\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Aoulef has a <a href=\"/wiki/Hot_desert_climate\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Hot desert climate\">hot desert climate</a> (<a href=\"/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification\" title=\"K\u00f6ppen climate classification\">K\u00f6ppen climate classification</a> <i>BWh</i>), with long, extremely hot summers and short, very warm winters. Daytime temperatures are known to consistently approach 50&#160;\u00b0C (122&#160;\u00b0F) in summer and nighttime temperatures routinely remain above 30&#160;\u00b0C (86&#160;\u00b0F). Average annual rainfall is extremely low, with only 10&#160;mm (0.39&#160;in). The sky is nearly always clear throughout the year and cloudy days are extremely rare. Aoulef is often quoted as one of the hottest spots worldwide.\n</p>\n<div style=\"width:85%;\">\n<div>\n<table class=\"wikitable mw-collapsible\" style=\"width:auto; text-align:center; line-height:1.2em;\">\n\n<tbody><tr>\n<th colspan=\"14\">Climate data for Aoulef (Reggane Station)\n</th></tr>\n<tr>\n<th scope=\"row\">Month\n</th>\n<th scope=\"col\">Jan\n</th>\n<th scope=\"col\">Feb\n</th>\n<th scope=\"col\">Mar\n</th>\n<th scope=\"col\">Apr\n</th>\n<th scope=\"col\">May\n</th>\n<th scope=\"col\">Jun\n</th>\n<th scope=\"col\">Jul\n</th>\n<th scope=\"col\">Aug\n</th>\n<th scope=\"col\">Sep\n</th>\n<th scope=\"col\">Oct\n</th>\n<th scope=\"col\">Nov\n</th>\n<th scope=\"col\">Dec\n</th>\n<th scope=\"col\" style=\"border-left-width:medium\">Year\n</th></tr>\n<tr style=\"text-align: center;\">\n<th scope=\"row\" style=\"height: 16px;\">Mean daily maximum \u00b0C (\u00b0F)\n</th>\n<td style=\"background: #FF860D;
