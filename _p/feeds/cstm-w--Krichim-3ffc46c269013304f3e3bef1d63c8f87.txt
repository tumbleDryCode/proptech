class=\"infobox-data\">03145</td></tr></tbody></table>\n<p><b>Krichim</b> (<a href=\"/wiki/Bulgarian_language\" title=\"Bulgarian language\">Bulgarian</a>: <span lang=\"bg\">\u041a\u0440\u0438\u0447\u0438\u043c</span> <span class=\"IPA nowrap\" lang=\"bg-Latn-fonipa\"><a href=\"/wiki/Help:IPA/Bulgarian\" title=\"Help:IPA/Bulgarian\">&#91;\u02c8krit\u0361\u0283im&#93;</a></span>) is a town in <a href=\"/wiki/Bulgaria\" title=\"Bulgaria\">Bulgaria</a>, located in the southwestern part of <a href=\"/wiki/Plovdiv_Province\" title=\"Plovdiv Province\">Plovdiv Province</a> close to <a href=\"/wiki/Perushtitsa\" title=\"Perushtitsa\">Perushtitsa</a>. It lies at the foot of the <a href=\"/wiki/Rhodopes\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Rhodopes\">Rhodopes</a>' northern slopes in the <a href=\"/wiki/Upper_Thracian_Lowlands\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Upper Thracian Lowlands\">plains of Thrace</a>, 20&#160;km southwest of <a href=\"/wiki/Plovdiv\" title=\"Plovdiv\">Plovdiv</a>. The river <a href=\"/wiki/Vacha_River\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Vacha River\">Vacha</a>, an important tributary of the <a href=\"/wiki/Maritsa\" title=\"Maritsa\">Maritsa</a>, runs through the town. Krichim is the only place in <a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Krichim_Municipality&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1\" class=\"new\" title=\"Krichim Municipality (page does not exist)\">Krichim Municipality</a>.\n</p><p>Inhabited since early <a href=\"/wiki/Byzantine_Empire\" title=\"Byzantine Empire\">Byzantine</a> times as evidenced by the remains of two ancient fortresses on both banks of the river in the beginning of its gorge, Krichim was contested by the Byzantine Empire and the Bulgarian Empire during most of the Middle Ages. It was conquered by <a href=\"/wiki/List_of_Bulgarian_monarchs\" title=\"List of Bulgarian monarchs\">Tsar</a> <a href=\"/wiki/Ivan_Asen_II\" title=\"Ivan Asen II\">Ivan Asen II</a> in 1230 and a stone inscription from this period is preserved, saying 'On this stone sat Tsar Asen, when he conquered Krichim'.\n</p><p>During the early <a href=\"/wiki/Bulgarian_National_Revival\" title=\"Bulgarian National Revival\">Bulgarian National Revival</a>, the village was one of the few in the region to have a monastery school. Two monasteries, one of the Holy Theotokos and one of St Vrach, worked during the <a href=\"/wiki/Ottoman_Empire\" title=\"Ottoman Empire\">Ottoman</a> rule and helped preserve the Bulgarian culture and language.\n</p>\n<meta property=\"mw:PageProp/toc\" />\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Demography\">Demography</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Krichim&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1\" title=\"Edit section: Demography\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>The town is populated by both <a href=\"/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Eastern Orthodox\">Eastern Orthodox</a> Bulgarians and (Muslim) Turks as well as <a href=\"/wiki/Pomaks\" title=\"Pomaks\">Pomaks</a> (Muslim Bulgarians) and <a href=\"/wiki/Muslim_Roma\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Muslim Roma\">Muslim Roma</a>.
