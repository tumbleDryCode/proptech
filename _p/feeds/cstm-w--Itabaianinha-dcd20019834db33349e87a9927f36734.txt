In 2020, its population was 42,166, and its area is 493&#160;km\u00b2.<sup id=\"cite_ref-1\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-1\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>1<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>Itabaianinha is noted for its large <a href=\"/wiki/Dwarfism\" title=\"Dwarfism\">dwarf</a> population. It is sometimes called \"a cidade dos an\u00f5es\" (\"the city of dwarfs\").<sup id=\"cite_ref-2\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-2\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>2<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> The dwarfism is of the proportionate type and arises from a rare genetic mutation.<sup id=\"cite_ref-3\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> The population probably originated through <a href=\"/wiki/Consanguine_marriage\" title=\"Consanguine marriage\">intrafamily marriages</a>. The condition has been shown<sup id=\"cite_ref-4\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-4\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>4<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> to have no effect on normal lifespan. In the late 1990s many residents of Itabaianinha under 15 years started receiving hormonal treatment. The population of dwarfs is dwindling, with only an estimated one hundred remaining, mostly in the village of Carret\u00e9is, ten miles from Itabaianinha center.<sup id=\"cite_ref-5\"
