Eleven pits were owned by the <a href=\"/wiki/Aniche_Mining_Company\" title=\"Aniche Mining Company\">Aniche Mining Company</a> and three by the <i>Compagnie des mines d'Azincourt</i> (Agincourt Mining Company).\n</p>\n<meta property=\"mw:PageProp/toc\" />\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"History\">History</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Aniche&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1\" title=\"Edit section: History\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading3\"><h3 id=\"Middle_Ages\"><a href=\"/wiki/Middle_Ages\" title=\"Middle Ages\">Middle Ages</a></h3><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Aniche&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2\" title=\"Edit section: Middle Ages\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<ul><li>In the early 12th century, <a href=\"/wiki/Marchiennes_Abbey\" title=\"Marchiennes Abbey\">Marchiennes Abbey</a> owned the altar and tithes which had previously been owned by Anselm le Barbu, Count of Ostrevant, and his successors<sup id=\"cite_ref-4\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-4\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>4<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>\n<li>On 27 April 1181, Philippe, Count of Flanders and <a href=\"/wiki/Vermandois\" title=\"Vermandois\">Vermandois</a>, was at the Saint-Martin of Aniche basilica in the presence of the relics of the Virgin Eusebia. He regulated the rights of the Abbey on tithing.</li>\n<li>In 1209, Bauduin of Obrechicourt, knight, waived his rights for tithes from Aniche.</li>\n<li>In 1219, Robert, sire of Aniche, made an award in favour of the Abbey for land that he contested with Jean le Mirail d'Aniche.</li>\n<li>In 1340, during the <a href=\"/wiki/Hundred_Years_War\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Hundred Years War\">Hundred Years War</a>, the people of Douai, France's allies, burned the village which was considered pro-British.</li></ul>\n<p>Aniche and <a href=\"/wiki/Auberchicourt\" the last pit in 1938 and the final closure of the glassworks at the beginning of the <a href=\"/wiki/Second_World_War\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Second World War\">Second World There is also the D645 from <a href=\"/wiki/Auberchicourt\" title=\"Auberchicourt\">Auberchicourt</a> in the west passing through the town and continuing east to <a href=\"/wiki/Abscon\" title=\"Abscon\">Abscon</a>. More than half of the commune consists of the urban area of Aniche town with the rest of the commune in the east and south farmland.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Google_13-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Google-13\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>13<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading3\"><h3 id=\"Geology_and_terrain\">Geology and terrain</h3><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Aniche&amp;action=edit&amp;section=9\" title=\"Edit section: Geology and terrain\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>On 27 December 1893, an <a href=\"/wiki/Earthquake\" title=\"Earthquake\">earthquake</a> occurred in the glass-making district<sup id=\"cite_ref-14\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-14\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>14<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> which indicated a seismic risk that the coal mines then more or less took into account.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading3\"><h3 id=\"Communications_and_transport\">Communications and transport</h3><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Aniche&amp;action=edit&amp;section=10\" title=\"Edit section: Communications and transport\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Aniche is connected to Douai by bus route 1 (Aniche-Guesnain) which connects with Tram A (Guesnain-Douai) of the SMTC of Douai, as well as routes 211 (Guesnain-Denain via Aniche), 210 (Aniche-Somain), and 201 (Aniche-Villeneuve d'Ascq) operated by the Arc-en-Ciel network.\n</p><p>Phase 3 of Route A will link Aniche on the \u00c9v\u00e9ole network of autobuses to Douai.
