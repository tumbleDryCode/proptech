class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"History\">History</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Bombarral&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1\" title=\"Edit section: History\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<figure class=\"mw-halign-left\" typeof=\"mw:File/Thumb\"><a href=\"/wiki/File:Batalha_da_Roli%C3%A7a.jpg\" class=\"mw-file-description\"><img src=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/bc/Batalha_da_Roli%C3%A7a.jpg/250px-Batalha_da_Roli%C3%A7a.jpg\" decoding=\"async\" width=\"220\" height=\"151\" class=\"mw-file-element\" valley</figcaption></figure>\n<p>It is known that the area of Bombarral was occupied during the geological period, and there exist vestiges of human settlement throughout the municipality, including pre-historic remnants in <i>Gruta Nova</i>, <i>Lapa do Su\u00e3o</i> and the <a href=\"/wiki/Neolithic\" title=\"Neolithic\">Neolithic</a> fortifications of <i>Columbeira</i> and the <i>Castro de S\u00e3o Mamede</i>.<sup id=\"cite_ref-CMHistoria_3-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-CMHistoria-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>Most documented references to Bombarral begin in the 14th century, when the area was under the dominion of the <a href=\"/wiki/Alcoba%C3%A7a_Monastery\" title=\"Alcoba\u00e7a Monastery\">Monastery of Alcoba\u00e7a</a>.\n</p><p>With the creation of Portugal, King <a href=\"/wiki/Afonso_Henriques\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Afonso Henriques\">Afonso Henriques</a> donated the lands to <a href=\"/wiki/Cistercian\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Cistercian\">Cistercian</a> monks, around 1153.<sup id=\"cite_ref-CMHistoria_3-1\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-CMHistoria-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> Before, the <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_of_Aljubarrota\" title=\"Battle of Aljubarrota\">Battle of Aljubarrota</a> King <a href=\"/wiki/John_I_of_Portugal\" title=\"John I of Portugal\">John of Portugal</a> stayed in Bombarral with his Knight, Lu\u00eds Henriques, in the strong-house that would later be converted into the municipality's council hall.<sup id=\"cite_ref-CMHistoria_3-2\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-CMHistoria-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>In the 18th century it was recognized as <i>\"Queens lands\"</i>, and part of the municipality of <a href=\"/wiki/Cadaval_Municipality\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Cadaval Municipality\">Cadaval</a> until 1852. During the <a href=\"/wiki/Peninsular_War\" title=\"Peninsular War\">Peninsular War</a>, 15000 Anglo-Portuguese troops confronted a much smaller army of French infantry and cavalry (5000 total) in <a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Roli%C3%A7a_(Bombarral)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1\" class=\"new\" title=\"Roli\u00e7a (Bombarral) (page does not exist)\">Roli\u00e7a</a> in the first engagement by British forces.<sup id=\"cite_ref-CMHistoria_3-3\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-CMHistoria-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_of_Roli%C3%A7a\" title=\"Battle of Roli\u00e7a\">Battle of Roli\u00e7a</a>, along the northern frontier with \u00d3bidos, was a confrontation between English General <a href=\"/wiki/Arthur_Wellesley,_1st_Duke_of_Wellington\" title=\"Arthur Wellesley, 1st Duke of Wellington\">Arthur Wellesley</a> and French General <a href=\"/wiki/Henri_Delaborde\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Henri Delaborde\">Henri Fran\u00e7ois, Comte Delaborde</a>, who were sent by General <a href=\"/wiki/Jean-Andoche_Junot\" title=\"Jean-Andoche Junot\">Jean-Andoche Junot</a>, to harass and hold British forces until a much larger force could confront the English. The valleys and gullies of Roli\u00e7a allowed the small French contingent to confront the much larger British force, yet they were unable to defeat them. Although successful, Wellesley did not press the fleeing French troops (who withdrew to Montachique near Torres Vedras), and instead went to support the landing of 4000 troops arriving from England along the coast.\n</p>\n<figure class=\"mw-default-size\" typeof=\"mw:File/Thumb\"><a href=\"/wiki/File:Bombarral_Station.jpg\" class=\"mw-file-description\"><img src=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e4/Bombarral_Station.jpg/250px-Bombarral_Station.jpg\" decoding=\"async\" width=\"220\" height=\"249\" class=\"mw-file-element\" srcset=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e4/Bombarral_Station.jpg/330px-Bombarral_Station.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e4/Bombarral_Station.jpg/500px-Bombarral_Station.jpg 2x\" data-file-width=\"1131\" data-file-height=\"1280\" /></a><figcaption>The Train Station in Bombarral</figcaption></figure>\n<p>The railway connecting Bombarral to <a href=\"/wiki/Lisbon\" title=\"Lisbon\">Lisbon</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Leiria\" title=\"Leiria\">Leiria</a> came in 1887, starting a period of accelerated economic development.\n</p><p><br />\n</p><p>Until 1914, Bombarral was a parish of the municipality of neighbouring <a href=\"/wiki/%C3%93bidos,_Portugal\" title=\"\u00d3bidos, Portugal\">\u00d3bidos</a>. The present-day municipality was created that year.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Geography\">Geography</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Bombarral&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2\" title=\"Edit section: Geography\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading3\"><h3 id=\"Physical_geography\">Physical geography</h3><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Bombarral&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3\" title=\"Edit section: Physical geography\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Bombarral is situated in a privileged geographic region in the extreme southern part of the District of Leiria, in the centre of Western Tourist Region (<a href=\"/wiki/Portuguese_language\" title=\"Portuguese language\">Portuguese</a>: <i lang=\"pt\">Regi\u00e3o de Turismo do Oeste</i>), 75 kilometres from Lisbon and 20 kilometres from the Atlantic Ocean.
