The urbanized part, the parish of <a href=\"/wiki/Abrantes_(S%C3%A3o_Vicente_e_S%C3%A3o_Jo%C3%A3o)_e_Alferrarede\" title=\"Abrantes (S\u00e3o Vicente e S\u00e3o Jo\u00e3o) e Alferrarede\">Abrantes (S\u00e3o Vicente e S\u00e3o Jo\u00e3o) e Alferrarede</a>, located on the north bank of the Tagus, has about 17,000 residents.\n</p>\n<meta property=\"mw:PageProp/toc\" />\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"History\">History</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Abrantes&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1\" /></a><figcaption>The detention block, used to house prisoners, located within the walls of the Castle of Abrantes</figcaption></figure>\n<p>The <a href=\"/wiki/Celts\" title=\"Celts\">Celts</a> are believed to have established the first settlement in Abrantes around 300 BC. The name is derived from <a href=\"/wiki/Latin\" title=\"Latin\">Latin</a> <i>Aurantes</i>, perhaps referring to deposits of <a href=\"/wiki/Alluvial\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Alluvial\">alluvial</a> <a href=\"/wiki/Gold\" title=\"Gold\">gold</a> (Latin: <i><a href=\"https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/aurum\" class=\"extiw\" title=\"wiktionary:aurum\">aurum</a></i>) along the Tagus. Similarly, <a href=\"/wiki/Roman_art\" title=\"Roman art\">Roman</a> mosaics, coins, the remains of ancient aqueducts, as well as other antiquities, have been discovered in the vicinity of Abrantes.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Chisholm_3-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Chisholm-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>The village of Abrantes and the <a href=\"/wiki/Abrantes_Castle\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Abrantes Castle\">Abrantes Castle</a> were conquered from the <a href=\"/wiki/Al-Andalus\" title=\"Al-Andalus\">Moors</a> by <a href=\"/wiki/Afonso_I_of_Portugal\" title=\"Afonso I of Portugal\">Afonso I of Portugal</a> in 1148. After 1172, Abrantes was a military outpost, under the dominion of the <a href=\"/wiki/Order_of_Saint_James_of_Compostela\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Order of Saint James of Compostela\">Order of Saint James of Compostela</a>. In 1173, the region, including its <a href=\"/wiki/Abrantes_Castle\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Abrantes Castle\">castle</a>, were donated to the <a href=\"/wiki/Order_of_Saint_James_of_the_Sword\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Order of Saint James of the Sword\">Order of Saint James of the Sword</a>. Around 1290, <a href=\"/wiki/Pope_Nicholas_IV\" title=\"Pope Nicholas IV\">Pope Nicholas IV</a> released this Order from the jurisdiction of the Castilian grand master of Santiago de Compostela Order in <a href=\"/wiki/Ucl%C3%A9s\" title=\"Ucl\u00e9s\">Ucl\u00e9s</a>. It received its municipal <a href=\"/wiki/Foral\" title=\"Foral\">foral</a> in 1179, in compensation for its resistance against the Moroccan Abem Jacob. Its creation in 1179, was based on the <i>concelhos perfeitos</i> (\"perfect municipality\") model; they were formed on the specific day that settlers were invited to populate them; the administrative regime, the organization of the municipal authority and geographic distribution were planned as part of system of populating uninhabited lands, and extending the political influence of the Kingdom. Its geography contributed to the colonization:\n</p>\n<style data-mw-deduplicate=\"TemplateStyles:r1244412712\">.mw-parser-output .templatequote{overflow:hidden;margin:1em 0;padding:0 32px}.mw-parser-output .templatequotecite{line-height:1.5em;text-align:left;margin-top:0}@media(min-width:500px){.mw-parser-output .templatequotecite{padding-left:1.6em}}</style><blockquote class=\"templatequote\"><p>Zone of permanent conflicts, where the <a href=\"/wiki/Reconquista\" title=\"Reconquista\">Reconquista</a> came to pontual conclusion, the colonization of Monte Abrantino was facilitated by the geographic situation of the mount, situated to the north of the Tagus, which served as a natural frontier and protected it from incursions; the Tagus which was an important source of richness (water, fish, gold, navigation, etc.), a mount situated in the confluence and transition of territories, crossroads of trails between south and norther, free of overflows, with persistent fog, cleansed of good aires, all these were conditions that,...gave the location the conditions necessary to attract colonists, colonization that, it seems, not to have been easy...<sup id=\"cite_ref-4\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-4\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>4<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup></p></blockquote>\n<p>On 24 April 1281, King <a href=\"/wiki/Dinis_of_Portugal\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Dinis of Portugal\">Dinis of Portugal</a>, conceded the ownership of Abrantes to Queen <a href=\"/wiki/Elizabeth_of_Portugal\" title=\"Elizabeth of Portugal\">Elizabeth of Portugal</a>. <sup class=\"noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact\" style=\"white-space:nowrap;\">&#91;<i><a href=\"/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed\" title=\"Wikipedia:Citation needed\"><span title=\"This claim needs references to reliable sources. (November 2024)\">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>\n</p><p><a href=\"/wiki/Afonso_IV_of_Portugal\" title=\"Afonso IV of Portugal\">Afonso IV of Portugal</a> transferred the title of the religious parish to the <a href=\"/wiki/Knights_Hospitaller\" title=\"Knights Hospitaller\">Knights Hospitaller</a>, in 1327, and granted Abrantes to <a href=\"/wiki/Leonor_Teles\" title=\"Leonor Teles\">Leonor Teles</a>. Partners of the Master of Avis, the Abrantinos were one of the first to support the Lisboete movement of 1383, among them Fernando \u00c1lvares de Almeida, protector of the Casa de Abrantes. <sup class=\"noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact\" style=\"white-space:nowrap;\">&#91;<i><a href=\"/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed\" title=\"Wikipedia:Citation needed\"><span title=\"This claim needs references to reliable sources. (November 2024)\">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>\n</p><p><a href=\"/wiki/Manuel_I_of_Portugal\" title=\"Manuel I of Portugal\">King Manuel</a> stayed in Abrantes for a long period; by 1510 he conceded a new foral to the town; and his children, the Infantes Fernando and Lu\u00eds were born in Abrantes. During the 16th century, Abrantes and its religious parish was one of the largest and more populous lands in the Kingdom, with 3,436 inhabitants, four convents and 13 chapels or churches within its frontiers. <sup class=\"noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact\" style=\"white-space:nowrap;\">&#91;<i><a href=\"/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed\" title=\"Wikipedia:Citation needed\"><span title=\"This claim needs references to reliable sources. (November 2024)\">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>\n</p><p>In 1581, in his move to <a href=\"/wiki/Tomar\" title=\"Tomar\">Tomar</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Philip_I_of_Portugal\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Philip I of Portugal\">Philip I of Portugal</a> stopped in Abrantes for several days, and during his reign was responsible for the reconstruction of Abrantes' municipal buildings.\nIt was the seat of Portuguese marquesses, one of which was an early patron of the Brazilian-born versatile scientist, naturalist and pioneering inventor-aeronaut <a href=\"/wiki/Bartolomeu_de_Gusm%C3%A3o\" title=\"Bartolomeu de Gusm\u00e3o\">Bartholomeu Louren\u00e7o de Gusm\u00e3o</a>. <sup class=\"noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact\" style=\"white-space:nowrap;\">&#91;<i><a href=\"/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed\" title=\"Wikipedia:Citation needed\"><span title=\"This claim needs references to reliable sources. (November 2024)\">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>\n</p><p>During 1640, Abrantes was one of the first lands to declare their support for <a href=\"/wiki/John_IV_of_Portugal\" title=\"John IV of Portugal\">John IV of Portugal</a>. From the 17th and 18th centuries onwards, its ancient military importance was confirmed, as it was used by Portuguese and foreign armies to garrison or concentrate their forces. The village of Abrantes was captured on 24 November 1807 by French general <a href=\"/wiki/Jean-Andoche_Junot\" title=\"Jean-Andoche Junot\">Jean-Andoche Junot</a>, who was awarded a ducal title for his victory by <a href=\"/wiki/Napoleon_I\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Napoleon I\">Napoleon I</a> in 1808, and used as base for further marshaling of French forces.<sup id=\"cite_ref-5\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-5\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>5<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the <a href=\"/wiki/Convention_of_Cintra\" title=\"Convention of Cintra\">Convention of Cintra</a> (22 August 1808) the town was returned to the Portuguese.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Chisholm_3-1\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Chisholm-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>During the reign of <a href=\"/wiki/Joseph_I_of_Portugal\" title=\"Joseph I of Portugal\">Joseph I</a> (1750\u20131777) the village became the center of the silk industry, which prospered until 1800, designated the <i>Academia Tubuciana</i>. <sup class=\"noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact\" style=\"white-space:nowrap;\">&#91;<i><a href=\"/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed\" title=\"Wikipedia:Citation needed\"><span title=\"This claim needs references to reliable sources. (November 2024)\">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>\n</p><p>In 1820, Abrantes supported the Liberal Revolution and celebrated the 1822 Constitution; although <a href=\"/wiki/Miguel_of_Portugal\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Miguel of Portugal\">Miguel of Portugal</a> did find many supporters in the municipality, no outright manifestation of support occurred in light of the <i><a href=\"/wiki/Vilafrancada\" title=\"Vilafrancada\">Vilafrancada</a></i>. In November 1862, the Santar\u00e9m-Abrantes lines was opened, and by March 1868, a bridge between Abrantes and Rossio to the southern Tagus region was well underway. <sup class=\"noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact\" style=\"white-space:nowrap;\">&#91;<i><a href=\"/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed\" title=\"Wikipedia:Citation needed\"><span title=\"This claim needs references to reliable sources. (November 2024)\">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>\n</p><p>An active republican center, Abrantes was the place of preparatory meetings for the <a href=\"/wiki/5_October_1910_Revolution\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"5 October 1910 Revolution\">5 October 1910 Revolution</a>, which may have contributed to the village's elevation to the status of city on 14 June 1916. <sup class=\"noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact\" style=\"white-space:nowrap;\">&#91;<i><a href=\"/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed\" title=\"Wikipedia:Citation needed\"><span title=\"This claim needs references to reliable sources. Abrantes, one the historical roads of the old quarter, in the municipal seat of Abrantes</figcaption></figure>\n<p>Abrantes lies in a central enclave within the Portuguese territory and a transition zone between the <a href=\"/wiki/Ribatejo_Province\" title=\"Ribatejo Province\">Ribatejo</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Beira_Baixa_Province\" title=\"Beira Baixa Province\">Beira Baixa</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Alto_Alentejo_Province\" title=\"Alto Alentejo Province\">Alto Alentejo</a>. This location allowed development of a diversified culture, influenced by many cultures around it; <a href=\"/wiki/Sardoal\" title=\"Sardoal\">Sardoal</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Vila_de_Rei\" title=\"Vila de Rei\">Vila de Rei</a>, to the north; <a href=\"/wiki/Ma%C3%A7%C3%A3o\" title=\"Ma\u00e7\u00e3o\">Ma\u00e7\u00e3o</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Gavi%C3%A3o_(parish)\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Gavi\u00e3o (parish)\">Gavi\u00e3o</a>, to the east; <a href=\"/wiki/Ponte_de_S%C3%B4r\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Ponte de S\u00f4r\">Ponte de S\u00f4r</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Chamusca\" title=\"Chamusca\">Chamusca</a>, in the south; and <a href=\"/wiki/Const%C3%A2ncia\" title=\"Const\u00e2ncia\">Const\u00e2ncia</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Tomar\" title=\"Tomar\">Tomar</a> in the west. Abrantes is also located along many important road and rail links throughout the country, including the Madrid-Badajoz-Lisbon railway, being at the junction with the Guarda-Abrantes line, and the Lisbon-Porto access (the A23 and Beira Baixa roadways).\n</p><p>Abrantes commands the highway along the Tagus valley west from <a href=\"/wiki/Belver_(Gavi%C3%A3o)\" title=\"Belver (Gavi\u00e3o)\">Belver</a> (in the municipality of <a href=\"/wiki/Gavi%C3%A3o_Municipality\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Gavi\u00e3o Municipality\">Gavi\u00e3o</a>) until <a href=\"/wiki/Const%C3%A2ncia\" title=\"Const\u00e2ncia\">Const\u00e2ncia</a>, occupying the crest of a hill covered with olive woods, gardens and vines; historically, the fortified town has been regarded as an important military position.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Chisholm_3-2\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Chisholm-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>The municipality is also center of two major hydrological resources: the <a href=\"/wiki/Tagus_River\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Tagus River\">Tagus River</a>, which cross the territory for 30 kilometers and the Albufeira from <a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Castelo_do_Bode&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1\" class=\"new\" title=\"Castelo do Bode (page does not exist)\">Castelo do Bode</a>.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading3\"><h3 id=\"Climate\">Climate</h3><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Abrantes&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4\" title=\"Edit section: Climate\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Abrantes has a <a href=\"/wiki/Mediterranean_climate\" title=\"Mediterranean climate\">Mediterranean climate</a> with hot, dry summers and mild, wet winters. Its location in the <a href=\"/wiki/Tagus\" title=\"Tagus\">Tagus</a> <a href=\"/wiki/Floodplain\" title=\"Floodplain\">floodplain</a> enables very high <a href=\"/wiki/Diurnal_air_temperature_variation\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Diurnal air temperature variation\">diurnal temperature variations</a>; making nights cool, with an average yearly low of 8.7&#160;\u00b0C (47.7&#160;\u00b0F), and dropping below 0&#160;\u00b0C (32&#160;\u00b0F) 31 days on average during the cold months;<sup id=\"cite_ref-IPMA_6-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-IPMA-6\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>6<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup> and days being warm to hot, with an average yearly high of 23.3&#160;\u00b0C (73.9&#160;\u00b0F).\n</p>\n<div>\n<table class=\"wikitable mw-collapsible\" style=\"width:auto; text-align:center; line-height:1.2em;\">\n\n<tbody><tr>\n<th colspan=\"14\">Climate data for Abrantes
