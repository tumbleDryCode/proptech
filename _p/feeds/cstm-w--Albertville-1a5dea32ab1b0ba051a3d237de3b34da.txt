Its altitude ranges from 345 to 2,037 metres (1,132 to 6,683&#160;ft). Nearby <a href=\"/wiki/Mountain\" title=\"Mountain\">mountains</a> include: Belle \u00c9toile, <a href=\"/wiki/Dent_de_Cons\" title=\"Dent de Cons\">Dent de Cons</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/N%C3%A9gresse\" title=\"N\u00e9gresse\">N\u00e9gresse</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Roche_Pourrie\" title=\"Roche Pourrie\">Roche Pourrie</a>, Mirantin, <a href=\"/wiki/Pointe_de_la_Grande_Journ%C3%A9e\" title=\"Pointe de la Grande Journ\u00e9e\">Pointe de la Grande Journ\u00e9e</a>, Cha\u00eene du Grand Arc. Nearby <a href=\"/wiki/Mountain_range\" title=\"Mountain range\">mountain ranges</a> include the <a href=\"/wiki/Bauges\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Bauges\">Bauges</a>, the <a href=\"/wiki/Beaufortain\" title=\"Beaufortain\">Beaufortain</a> and the beginning of the <a href=\"/wiki/Vanoise_massif\" title=\"Vanoise massif\">Vanoise</a>.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"History\">History</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Albertville&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2\" title=\"Edit section: History\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<figure class=\"mw-default-size mw-halign-left\" typeof=\"mw:File/Thumb\"><a href=\"/wiki/File:Conflans01.jpg\" class=\"mw-file-description\"><img src=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/08/Conflans01.jpg/220px-Conflans01.jpg\" decoding=\"async\" width=\"220\" height=\"147\" class=\"mw-file-element\" srcset=\"//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/08/Conflans01.jpg/330px-Conflans01.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/08/Conflans01.jpg/440px-Conflans01.jpg 2x\" data-file-width=\"1536\" data-file-height=\"1024\" /></a><figcaption>Medieval town of Conflans, Albertville</figcaption></figure>\n<p>The modern city of Albertville was formed in 1836 by King <a href=\"/wiki/Charles_Albert_of_Sardinia\" title=\"Charles Albert of Sardinia\">Charles Albert of Sardinia</a>, who merged the medieval town of Conflans, which had buildings dating to the 14th century, with the town of L'H\u00f4pital. Since then, Albertville has fostered trade between France, Italy and Switzerland. Industries such as <a href=\"/wiki/Paper_mills\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Paper mills\">paper mills</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Hydroelectricity\" title=\"Hydroelectricity\">hydroelectricity</a> are found along its river.\n</p><p>The <a href=\"/wiki/1992_Winter_Olympics\" title=\"1992 Winter Olympics\">1992 Winter Olympics</a> were organised in the Savoie region, with Albertville hosting it. Some of the sports venues were later adapted for other uses. Some sports venues still remain, such as the <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_rink\" title=\"Ice rink\">ice rink</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/La_halle_de_glace_Olympique\" title=\"La halle de glace Olympique\">La halle de glace Olympique</a>, designed by the architect Jacques Kalisz. Despite this, the town remains more industrial than touristic.<sup id=\"cite_ref-4\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-4\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>4<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p><p>In 2003, the town was labelled a \"<a href=\"/wiki/French_Towns_and_Lands_of_Art_and_History\" title=\"French Towns and Lands of Art and History\">Town of Art and History</a>\".<sup class=\"noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact\" style=\"white-space:nowrap;\">&#91;<i><a href=\"/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed\" title=\"Wikipedia:Citation needed\"><span title=\"This claim needs references to reliable sources.
