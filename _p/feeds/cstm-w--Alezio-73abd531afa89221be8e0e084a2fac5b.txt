lang=\"cms\">Alytia</i>; <a href=\"/wiki/Latin_language\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Latin language\">Latin</a>: <i lang=\"la\">Aletium</i>) is a town and <i><a href=\"/wiki/Comune\" title=\"Comune\">comune</a></i> in the <a href=\"/wiki/Province_of_Lecce\" title=\"Province of Lecce\">province of Lecce</a> in the <a href=\"/wiki/Apulia\" title=\"Apulia\">Apulia</a> region of south-east <a href=\"/wiki/Italy\" title=\"Italy\">Italy</a>.\n</p>\n<meta property=\"mw:PageProp/toc\" />\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"History\">History</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Alezio&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1\" title=\"Edit section: History\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Alezio (despite legend assigning its foundation to a king of Crete) was a centre of the <a href=\"/wiki/Messapi\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Messapi\">Messapi</a>, who would call it <i><b>Alytia</b></i> like their former capital in <a href=\"/wiki/Acarnania\" title=\"Acarnania\">Acarnania</a>. According to <a href=\"/wiki/Pliny_the_Elder\" title=\"Pliny the Elder\">Pliny the Elder</a>, the Aletines descended instead from the <a href=\"/wiki/Iapyges\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Iapyges\">Iapyges</a>, who descended by the <a href=\"/wiki/Oscans\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Oscans\">Oscans</a>. It is mentioned as <i>Baletium</i> in the <a href=\"/wiki/Peutingerian_Table\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Peutingerian Table\">Peutingerian Table</a> (4th century AD). It was a stop on the <a href=\"/wiki/Via_Traiana\" title=\"Via Traiana\">Via Traiana</a>, who connected ancient Apulia to <a href=\"/wiki/Rome\" title=\"Rome\">Rome</a>.\n</p><p>Around the year 1000, Alezio was destroyed by the <a href=\"/wiki/Saracen\" title=\"Saracen\">Saracens</a>, its inhabitants moving to the <a href=\"/wiki/Gallipoli,_Apulia\" title=\"Gallipoli, Apulia\">Gallipoli</a> island. It remained deserted until the 12th-13th centuries, when a church dedicated to Santa Maria della Alizza or della Lizza was built by some <a href=\"/wiki/Basilian_monk\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Basilian monk\">Basilian monks</a>. The new <b>Casal d'Alezio</b>, built around it, remained a small village until the 18th century. It was called <b>Villa Picciotti</b> from the 18th century until 1873, when the Messapic name was restored.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Alezio_DOC\">Alezio DOC</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Alezio&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2\" title=\"Edit section: Alezio DOC\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Within Alezio is a 60-hectare (150-acre) region that produces red and <i><a href=\"/wiki/Rose_(wine)\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Rose (wine)\">rose</a></i> <a href=\"/wiki/DOC_wines\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"DOC wines\">DOC wines</a> that are a blend with up to 20% <a href=\"/wiki/Negroamaro\" title=\"Negroamaro\">Negroamaro</a>, plus <a href=\"/wiki/Sangiovese\" title=\"Sangiovese\">Sangiovese</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Malvasia\" title=\"Malvasia\">Malvasia</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Montepulciano_(grape)\" title=\"Montepulciano (grape)\">Montepulciano</a>. <a href=\"/wiki/Harvest_(wine)\" title=\"Harvest (wine)\">Harvest</a> <a href=\"/wiki/Yields_(wine)\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Yields (wine)\">yields</a> are limited to 14 tonnes/ha. The <a href=\"/wiki/Wines_are_aged\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Wines are aged\">wines are aged</a> for a minimum of 2 years prior to release and must contain a minimum alcohol level of 12.5%.<sup id=\"cite_ref-Saunders_pg_119_3-0\" class=\"reference\"><a href=\"#cite_note-Saunders_pg_119-3\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#91;</span>3<span class=\"cite-bracket\">&#93;</span></a></sup>\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Main_sights\">Main sights</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Alezio&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3\" title=\"Edit section: Main sights\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<ul><li>The Civic Museum of the Messapi, inaugurated in 1982, is a national monument of Italy. It houses remains from Messapic necropolises outside Alezio (4th-3rd centuries BC).</li>\n<li>The Sanctuary of Santa Maria della Lizza, usually considered to be built in the 12th-13th centuries, although remains of a Byzantine-style fresco could date its foundation to some two centuries before. The remains of the frescoes, which once covered the whole interior, span a range of time from the 10th and the 16th century. Most of them dealt with stories of the life of the Virgin Mary.</li>\n<li>Messapian Necropolis, an ancient messapian necropolis, located in Monte Delia area, found by archaeological excavations.</li></ul>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"Transportation\">Transportation</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a href=\"/w/index.php?title=Alezio&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4\" title=\"Edit section: Transportation\"><span>edit</span></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></div>\n<p>Alezio is served by a railway station of the <a href=\"/wiki/Ferrovie_Sud-Est\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Ferrovie Sud-Est\">Ferrovie Sud-Est</a>, which also holds several bus services in the area.\n</p>\n<div class=\"mw-heading mw-heading2\"><h2 id=\"References\">References</h2><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a
